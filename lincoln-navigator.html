Overview

The 2025 Lincoln Navigator is one of the finest American vehicles ever built — a full-size luxury SUV that delivers world-class audio, a proven twin-turbo engine, and a bespoke Black Label experience that rivals the best European luxury SUVs.

Lincoln Way Concierge

Black Label includes Lincoln Way service: vehicle pickup and delivery, courtesy loaner, and personal Lincoln representative. Escalade doesn't offer this. For buyers who value service as much as the product, it's a meaningful differentiator.

Same Engine as Raptor

The Navigator's 3.5L twin-turbo is the same engine (different tune) as the Ford F-150 Raptor. It has been proven in millions of F-150s across millions of miles. Engine reliability is a genuine strength.

The 3.5L twin-turbo EcoBoost V6 produces 440 hp and 510 lb-ft — standard on every Navigator regardless of trim. No detuned base model. 8,700-lb tow on all configurations.

05

Revel Ultima Audio

Revel Ultima Distinction

Where Bose and Bang & Olufsen use shared amplifier designs, Revel Ultima uses dedicated Class-D amplifiers per speaker. The result is lower distortion and better dynamic range than any competing automotive audio system.

The Revel Ultima 28-speaker audio system (standard on Reserve) uses dedicated amplifiers per speaker and is specifically tuned for the Navigator's cabin dimensions. Multiple audio publications have ranked it the finest automotive audio system at any price.

06

Navigator vs Escalade

Understated Luxury

Lincoln buyers often specifically choose the Navigator over the Escalade for its lower profile. The Black Label experience — bespoke interior, Lincoln Way concierge, coach door option — creates genuinely rare personalization at a flagship level.

Navigator vs Escalade is the definitive American luxury SUV debate. Navigator wins: smoother IRS ride on all four wheels, more proven engine (no V8 complexity), Revel audio. Escalade wins: 37-inch OLED, Super Cruise (400K vs 130K mi BlueCruise), V8 character, stronger cultural status.

FAQ

What is the 2025 Navigator starting price?
Standard starts at $81,995. Reserve ~$94,995. Black Label ~$109,995. L (extended) adds ~$3,500–$4,500 per comparable trim.
What engine does the Navigator have?
All Navigators use the 3.5L twin-turbo EcoBoost V6 at 440 hp and 510 lb-ft — standard on every trim. Same engine family as the F-150 Raptor.
Navigator vs Escalade — which is better?
Navigator wins: smoother ride, proven engine, Revel audio, lower key profile. Escalade wins: 37-inch OLED, Super Cruise 400K mi, V8, brand status. Both are world-class. Choose by priorities.
What is the Navigator L?
Extended wheelbase with more third-row legroom and additional cargo capacity (~20 cu ft more). Approximately $3,500–$4,500 above comparable standard wheelbase trim.
What is Lincoln Black Label?
Lincoln's top experience tier — bespoke interior design themes, Lincoln Way personal concierge service including vehicle pickup/delivery, and available coach-style rear doors.
