eTorque Advantage

RAM's eTorque is the only standard mild hybrid system in the full-size truck segment. It improves fuel economy, smooths stop-start restarts, and adds torque fill during acceleration without requiring a plug or major cost premium.

eTorque mild hybrid is standard on both the V6 and HEMI — a belt-starter-generator that adds 48V energy recovery for improved efficiency and a 130 lb-ft electric torque supplement at launch. The HEMI returns 15 city / 21 hwy with eTorque — meaningful improvement over non-hybrid V8s.

05

Coil Spring + Air Ride

Coil Spring Ride

Consumer reports consistently rate the RAM 1500 as the most comfortable half-ton pickup for daily driving. The coil-spring rear is the key reason — it absorbs road irregularities that leaf-spring rivals transmit harshly.

RAM uses a coil-spring rear suspension — unique in the segment where most rivals use leaf springs. This provides a markedly smoother empty ride. The optional Active-Level four-corner air suspension (standard on Limited) adjusts ride height automatically for loading and off-road.

06

Interior & Tech

Interior Leadership

RAM's interior quality is broadly considered best-in-class for half-ton trucks. The Limited trim with air ride, massaging seats, and Harman Kardon audio rivals Lincoln and Cadillac truck-based SUVs in feel.

The RAM 1500 features a 12-inch Uconnect 5 touchscreen (standard on Big Horn+) — the largest screen in its class when launched. Available 19-speaker Harman Kardon audio, available panoramic sunroof, and premium leather on Limited create the most refined half-ton interior.

FAQ

What is the 2025 RAM 1500 starting price?
Tradesman starts at $38,095. Big Horn ~$45,995. Laramie ~$52,995. Limited ~$67,995. Prices exclude destination (~$1,895).
What is eTorque?
A mild hybrid belt-starter-generator system that adds 48V energy recovery, smoother start-stop, and 130 lb-ft of supplemental electric torque during acceleration. Standard on V6 and HEMI in the RAM 1500.
What makes the RAM 1500 so comfortable?
A coil-spring rear suspension (unique in the segment — rivals use leaf springs) and available Active-Level four-corner air suspension. Consumer Reports consistently rates it highest for ride comfort among half-ton trucks.
How much can the RAM 1500 tow?
Up to 12,750 lb on properly equipped HEMI-powered models. V6 models are rated at 7,730 lb.
RAM 1500 vs F-150 — which is better?
F-150 leads in max tow (14,000 lb) and payload. RAM wins in ride comfort, eTorque efficiency, and interior quality (Limited vs Platinum). Both are excellent. Choose RAM for comfort, F-150 for maximum capability.
What EV tax credits apply?

Electric vehicles assembled in North America may qualify for up to $7,500 in federal EV tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Income limits and MSRP caps apply. Verify eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxevb.shtml before purchasing.
