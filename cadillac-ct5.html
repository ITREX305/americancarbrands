vs BMW M5

CT5-V Blackwing: $94,990, 668 hp, manual available. BMW M5: $115,000+, 617 hp, no manual. For driving purists who want the engagement of a manual at max power, there is no alternative to the Blackwing anywhere in the world.

The CT5-V Blackwing's supercharged 6.2L V8 delivers 668 hp and 659 lb-ft — the most powerful Cadillac sedan ever built. Magnetic Ride Control 4.0, Brembo 6-piston front brakes, and an available 6-speed manual create a driving experience no European competitor matches at the price.

Daily Luxury

33-Inch Screen Standard

The CT5 Premium Luxury and above feature the 33-inch curved display — standard. At $52,990, this makes it arguably the best-value luxury sedan for screen-focused buyers.

The base CT5 with the 2.0L turbo is a refined daily sedan — the 33-inch curved display is class-leading, the interior quality above average for the segment, and fuel economy at 22/32 mpg FWD is reasonable.

06

Manual at 668 HP

Cultural Statement

Offering a manual transmission in a 668-hp sedan is Cadillac saying: we build cars for drivers, not just passengers. This single feature has generated more positive automotive press coverage than any other Cadillac decision in a decade.

The 6-speed manual Blackwing is a deliberate product decision by Cadillac — acknowledging that driving enthusiasts want the engagement of a clutch pedal, regardless of how fast the auto is. Porsche dropped the manual from the 911 Turbo. BMW dropped it from M5. Cadillac kept it, and enthusiasts around the world have noticed.

FAQ

What is the 2025 CT5 starting price?
Luxury starts at $40,990. Premium Luxury ~$52,990. CT5-V ~$65,990. CT5-V Blackwing ~$94,990. Prices exclude destination (~$1,095).
What is the Blackwing?
The CT5-V Blackwing is Cadillac's ultimate performance sedan — 668 hp supercharged V8, available 6-speed manual, Brembo brakes, and Magnetic Ride Control. Fastest production Cadillac sedan ever built.
Does it have a manual transmission?
Only the Blackwing — available with a 6-speed manual Tremec transmission. No other production sedan at this power level offers a manual in 2025.
CT5-V Blackwing vs BMW M5?
Blackwing: $94,990, 668 hp, manual available, American character. M5: $115,000+, 617 hp, no manual. Track times are comparable. Choose based on brand preference and manual vs automatic.
Is the CT5 reliable?
Generally yes. V6 models especially so. Blackwing requires premium fuel and supercharger belt service. Electronics are the primary area of concern but improving with OTA updates.

Verdict

The CT5-V Blackwing costs $18–$26K less than its European rivals while delivering more horsepower and the only available manual transmission in the class. BMW M5 wins on all-weather AWD grip and brand prestige. Mercedes-AMG E63 wins on interior refinement. The Blackwing wins on value, character, and driver engagement — especially with the 6-speed manual. For buyers who prioritize the driving experience over the badge, the Blackwing is the rational choice.
