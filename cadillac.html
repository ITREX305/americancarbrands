Brand Overview

Cadillac isn't just a car brand — it's the original American Standard of the World. Founded in 1902 by precision engineer Henry Leland, Cadillac invented the electric starter in 1912, pioneered the synchromesh transmission, and set the benchmark for American luxury that every premium brand still chases today. Acquired by General Motors in 1909, it became the ride of presidents, celebrities, and anyone who demanded the absolute best Detroit engineering could deliver.

In 2026 Cadillac is writing its boldest chapter yet. The LYRIQ delivers up to 314 miles of range with class-leading Super Cruise hands-free driving. At the pinnacle sits the hand-built CELESTIQ — a bespoke ultra-luxury EV starting at ~$340,000 that takes six months to craft. The Escalade IQ EV brings electric luxury to the full-size SUV segment at ~$130,000 with 450+ miles projected range.

Super Cruise Technology

Super Cruise is Cadillac's semi-autonomous hands-free driving technology using LiDAR-mapped roads, GPS, cameras, and an infrared driver attention system. It enables true hands-free highway driving on 400,000+ mapped miles across North America — the broadest coverage of any mass-market system.

Maintenance & Common Issues

Known issues and service intervals for current Cadillac models. Based on owner reports, NHTSA complaints, and independent mechanic data. Always verify with a certified technician.

Air Suspension (Escalade)
Compressor failures after ~80K miles. Repair cost: $1,200–$2,500. Recommend extended warranty.
OLED Display (Escalade)
Intermittent software glitches resolved by OTA updates. Physical failure rare but expensive (~$3,000+).
Infotainment (XT5/XT6)
Pre-2024 builds prone to freezing. Updated software largely resolves; verify OTA status before buying used.
Magnetic Ride Shock Absorbers
Typical life 70K–90K miles. Replacement ~$1,500–$2,500/axle. Standard interval: inspect at 60K.
Super Cruise Subscription
~$25/month ongoing after trial. Budget into total ownership cost.
Recommended Service Intervals

Oil change: every 7,500–10,000 miles (full synthetic). Transmission fluid: 45,000 miles. Brake fluid: 2 years. Cabin air filter: 15,000 miles. Verify at cadillac.com/owners.

FAQ

What is Super Cruise?
Super Cruise uses LiDAR map data, GPS, cameras, and driver attention monitoring to enable hands-free driving on 400,000+ mapped highway miles. Available on Escalade Platinum/V-Series, CT5 Platinum, LYRIQ, and CELESTIQ as standard. Requires subscription (~$25/month) after trial.
Is the Cadillac LYRIQ worth buying in 2026?
The LYRIQ offers 314-mile EPA range, Super Cruise, and luxury appointments at ~$59,990 — competitive with BMW iX and Mercedes EQE. Early reliability surveys are positive. Check federal EV tax credit eligibility at fueleconomy.gov. Verify current pricing at cadillac.com.
How reliable is Cadillac in 2026?
Cadillac ranked 4th overall in J.D. Power 2026 VDS (175 PP100). Gas models (Escalade, XT5) show mixed CR scores, primarily on electronics. The LYRIQ's early reliability data is positive. Always verify current ratings at consumerreports.org and jdpower.com.
What is the Escalade IQ?
The Escalade IQ is Cadillac's all-electric full-size SUV starting ~$130,000+. Ultium platform, ~450 miles EPA projected, Super Cruise standard. Deliveries began 2025. Verify availability at cadillac.com.
How does Cadillac compare to BMW and Mercedes-Benz?
Cadillac typically prices 10–20% below equivalent German luxury competitors. Super Cruise is generally regarded as more capable than BMW's hands-free system on US roads. Long-term reliability historically lagged German brands but the gap has narrowed in recent J.D. Power data.
What should I check before buying a used Cadillac?
1. Run VIN at nhtsa.gov for open recalls. 2. Verify Super Cruise subscription status. 3. Check air suspension (Escalade) and infotainment for reported issues. 4. Get an independent pre-purchase inspection. 5. Confirm pricing at CarGurus or AutoTrader for current market values.
