The most searched questions about the F-150 — answered with data.

Is the Ford F-150 expensive to insure?+

The F-150 averages $148/month ($1,776/year) for full coverage — $19/month below the national average for full-size trucks. The F-150's high parts availability and wide dealer network keep repair costs and premiums lower than less common vehicles.

How long does an F-150 last?+

A properly maintained Ford F-150 will last 200,000–300,000 miles. The 5.0L V8 and 2.7L EcoBoost are the most durable powertrains, with many examples exceeding 250,000 miles. Regular oil changes (every 7,500 miles with synthetic) and spark plug replacement at 60,000 miles are key.

Which F-150 engine is best?+

The 2.7L EcoBoost V6 is the best overall choice — excellent power (325 hp), best proven reliability record, and good fuel economy (~22 mpg hwy). The PowerBoost Hybrid is best for fuel savings if you drive high miles. The 5.0L V8 is the most satisfying if sound and simplicity matter to you.

How does F-150 compare to Chevy Silverado?+

The F-150 outsells the Silverado by ~200K units/year. F-150 advantages: better resale value, wider dealer network, more available trims. Silverado advantages: stronger 6.2L V8 option, GM's longer powertrain warranty in some trims. On reliability, both are average — choose on features and dealer proximity.
