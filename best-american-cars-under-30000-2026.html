FAQ
What is the cheapest new American car in 2026?
The Chevrolet Trax LS at $21,700 MSRP — the lowest-priced American-brand new vehicle. Add $1,495 destination and 6% representative tax: approximately $24,500 out-the-door.
Is the Ford Maverick still under $30,000 in 2026?
The Maverick XL Hybrid FWD starts at $28,145 as of March 2026 — still technically under $30,000 at base MSRP. However, once destination ($1,595) is added, the pre-tax price is $29,740 — right at the ceiling. Out-the-door including tax: ~$31,400.
What happened to the Chevy Colorado under $30,000?
The Colorado WT has risen to $32,400 as of March 2026 — well above the $30K ceiling. It is no longer a genuine under-$30K vehicle for new buyers.
