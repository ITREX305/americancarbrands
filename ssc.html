Brand Overview

SSC North America was founded in 1998 by Jerod Shelby (no relation to Carroll Shelby or Shelby American). The company's first vehicle, the Ultimate Aero, achieved a Guinness World Record in 2007 with a two-way average speed of 256.14 mph — surpassing the Bugatti Veyron. The record was later broken by the Bugatti Veyron Super Sport in 2010.

The Tuatara: SSC's current flagship is the Tuatara, powered by a flat-plane-crank 5.9L twin-turbo V8 producing 1,350 hp on 91-octane and 1,750 hp on E85. A 2020 speed run generated controversy when a claimed 331 mph average was disputed and later confirmed at 282.9 mph for the single-direction run. SSC remains actively pursuing officially documented top speed records. Production: 100 Tuataras planned total. Verify current availability at sscnorthamerica.com.

Buying Tips 2026

Purchasing an SSC Tuatara involves direct engagement with SSC North America. With under 100 total planned units, availability is extremely limited. Prospective buyers should visit the Richland, Washington facility, review build documentation, and engage with SSC's factory directly. The speed record controversy of 2020 should be considered in context — independent timing has verified high (if not world-record) speeds, and SSC's engineering capability is legitimate. Parts and service require factory coordination.

Before You Buy — Checklist

1. Engage directly with SSC North America via sscnorthamerica.com for current production availability. 2. Request full build documentation and delivery timeline in writing. 3. Plan for factory-direct service — no independent service network exists. 4. Verify insurance with specialist providers experienced with hypercar coverage. 5. Understand E85 fuel requirements for maximum output — fuel availability matters for daily usability.

FAQ

Is SSC North America related to Carroll Shelby?
No. SSC North America was founded by Jerod Shelby — no family or business relation to Carroll Shelby or Shelby American. The name similarity has caused persistent confusion, but the companies are entirely separate.
What is the top speed of the SSC Tuatara?
SSC has documented a single-direction run of 282.9 mph in 2020. A two-way average top speed — required for Guinness records — has been more difficult to document officially. SSC continues to pursue a fully certified record. The car's theoretical capability based on power and aerodynamics supports 300+ mph.
How many SSC Tuataras will be made?
SSC plans to produce approximately 100 Tuataras total — a combination of standard and Aggressor variants. As of 2025, approximately 30 units have been delivered.
What engine does the SSC Tuatara use?
The Tuatara uses a bespoke 5.9L flat-plane-crank twin-turbocharged V8 engine producing 1,350 hp on 91-octane gasoline or 1,750 hp on E85 ethanol fuel. The engine was co-developed with Nelson Racing Engines.
Where is SSC North America located?
SSC North America is headquartered and manufactures in Richland, Washington — part of the Tri-Cities metropolitan area in southeastern Washington State.
