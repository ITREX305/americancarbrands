History & Background

SSC North America (originally Shelby SuperCars, unrelated to Carroll Shelby) was founded by Jerod Shelby in 1998 in Washington State. The Ultimate Aero set the Guinness World Record for fastest production car in 2007. The Tuatara, unveiled in 2011 and revised into production in 2019, is SSC's flagship hypercar. A 2020 record run video showing 331mph was later retracted due to instrumentation issues; the verified record of 282.9mph was set in 2021.

Corporate Structure

Ownership & Corporate Structure

SSC North America is a private company owned by Jerod Shelby. It operates from Richland, Washington. The company is one of the smallest production hypercar manufacturers in the world, building entirely to order.

Common Questions

SSC North America FAQ

Frequently asked questions about SSC North America. Independent editorial answers — not sponsored.

Is SSC related to Shelby American? +
No. SSC was originally called 'Shelby SuperCars' but Jerod Shelby (the founder) has no family or business relationship with Carroll Shelby's company. The name caused confusion and SSC rebranded to 'SSC North America' to clarify the distinction.
What happened with the 331mph record? +
SSC released a video in 2020 claiming 331mph, which would have been a new world record. Analysis by independent experts including Johnny Bohmer Proving Grounds revealed instrumentation errors (GPS showed different speeds on GPS vs claimed). SSC retracted the claim and conducted a new verified run in 2021, officially recording 282.9mph one-way.
Can you buy an SSC Tuatara? +
Yes — SSC is currently delivering Tuatara units to pre-order customers. 100 total examples are planned. Some have been delivered to customers in the US and internationally.
Is SSC related to Shelby American?+
No. SSC North America was founded by Jerod Shelby — no relation to Carroll Shelby or Shelby American. The shared last name is coincidental. SSC (originally Shelby SuperCars, now SSC North America) is based in West Richland, Washington, and is entirely independent.
What happened with the 331mph record?+
SSC initially claimed 331.15mph in October 2020. Subsequent analysis revealed GPS data and video inconsistencies suggesting the speed was miscalculated. SSC retracted the claim and conducted a new verified run, achieving a confirmed 282.9mph (two-way average) — which is still an extraordinary achievement. The company was transparent about the error.
How many SSC Tuataras are being made?+
SSC plans a total production run of approximately 100 Tuataras (coupes and roadsters combined). Each is hand-built to order. As of 2026, some units have been delivered to customers and several more are in various stages of production. Lead times are significant for a vehicle built in such small quantities.
How does SSC compare to Bugatti and Koenigsegg?+
Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ achieved 304.7mph (one-direction); Koenigsegg Agera RS holds 277.9mph (two-way average). SSC Tuatara at 282.9mph places it between Koenigsegg and Bugatti in verified records. SSC has more horsepower than most competitors but less heritage and a smaller service network than European hypercar makers.
