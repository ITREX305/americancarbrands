Brand Overview

Don Panoz founded Panoz Auto Development Company in 1989, drawing on the family's pharmaceutical business success (Élan Corporation). The Panoz Esperante — initially built with an aluminum chassis and Ford 4.6L V8 — established the brand's identity: a front-engine, rear-wheel-drive sports car prioritizing feel and driver engagement over outright performance numbers. Panoz became the first manufacturer to use an aluminum space frame and carbon composite body in an affordable production sports car.

Racing legacy: Panoz has competed at Le Mans multiple times, winning its class in 1999. The company's racing efforts through the American Le Mans Series (ALMS) built substantial motorsport credibility. Current: The Avezzano, launched in 2015, uses a supercharged Ford 5.0L V8 producing 430 hp in a lightweight tubular steel and aluminum chassis. Production is extremely limited — verify availability directly at panoz.com. Roadster Variant: A roadster version of the Avezzano expands open-air options.

FAQ

Is Panoz still making cars?
Yes, as of 2026. Panoz produces the Avezzano in extremely limited numbers from its Hoschton, Georgia facility. Production is so limited that direct factory engagement is typically required for purchase.
What is the Panoz Esperante?
The Esperante was Panoz's primary production model from approximately 2000 to 2007. It used Ford V8 power (4.6L and later 5.0L), an aluminum-intensive lightweight chassis, and a distinctive long-hood, front-engine roadster body. It's considered the classic Panoz and commands a growing collector following.
Did Panoz race at Le Mans?
Yes. Panoz competed at the 24 Hours of Le Mans multiple times through the late 1990s and 2000s, winning the GTS class in 1999. The American Le Mans Series (ALMS) was a central focus of Panoz's racing program.
Is Panoz related to the Irish pharmaceutical company?
Indirectly. Don Panoz's wealth came from the pharmaceutical company Élan Corporation, which he co-founded. His son Dan Panoz has been more involved in the automotive and motorsports businesses. The Panoz automotive company is a separate entity from pharmaceutical ventures.
What engine does the Panoz Avezzano use?
The Avezzano uses a supercharged Ford 5.0L V8 engine producing approximately 430 hp. The Ford-sourced powertrain provides a practical advantage for service — Ford dealers can handle engine maintenance, while chassis and body work requires Panoz specialist attention.
