Brand Intelligence

History & Background

Dan Panoz founded Panoz Motorsports in 1989 initially as a racing operation. The first road car, the Roadster, launched in 1992 using a Ford 5.0 V8. The company also competes at international endurance events including the 24 Hours of Le Mans with factory entries. Panoz is one of the few American brands with genuine Le Mans factory racing history outside of Ford and Corvette Racing.

Corporate Structure

Ownership & Corporate Structure

Panoz is privately held by the Panoz family. Operations are based in Hoschton, Georgia where vehicles are hand-built. The company runs on very low production volumes and maintains a racing operation alongside road car production.

Common Questions

Panoz FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Panoz. Independent editorial answers — not sponsored.

Is Panoz still in business? +
Yes — Panoz continues to produce road cars on a very limited basis from its Georgia facility. Production is measured in dozens per year rather than thousands.
Has Panoz raced at Le Mans? +
Yes. Panoz ran factory GTR-1 and LMP cars at Le Mans in the late 1990s and early 2000s. The Esperante GTLM also competed in IMSA and international GT racing. Racing heritage is central to the brand identity.
Where are Panoz cars built? +
All Panoz vehicles are hand-built at the Panoz Manufacturing facility in Hoschton, Georgia, USA.

📋 How to Check Panoz Recalls by VIN +
  1. Go to nhtsa.gov/recalls
  2. Enter your 17-digit VIN (found on dashboard, door jamb, or title)
  3. Review all open recalls — note which have been remediated
  4. Contact an authorized Panoz dealer to complete any open recalls at no cost
ⓘ Recall repairs are always free at authorized dealers, regardless of vehicle age, mileage, or ownership status.
📱 OTA vs Dealer Recall Repair — What’s the Difference? +
📱 OTA Update
Software-only fix pushed wirelessly. No dealer visit required. Common on Tesla, GM (Super Cruise), Ford (Sync), and newer models.
🔧 Dealer Repair
Physical parts replacement or mechanical repair required. Must schedule dealer appointment. Always free under recall.

ⓘ NHTSA determines recall remedy type. OTA-resolved recalls still appear in NHTSA history — check that your vehicle's remedy has been applied.
