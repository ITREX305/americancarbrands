Overview

Panoz has competed at Le Mans and in American sports car racing since the 1990s, building a reputation among enthusiasts for genuine motorsport engineering rather than marketing-driven performance claims. The Avezzano represents the company's current road car offering, translating decades of racing development directly into a street-legal sports car built in small numbers in Hoschton, Georgia.

The naturally aspirated V8 reflects Panoz's philosophy that genuine driving engagement comes from a responsive, predictable power delivery rather than maximizing peak horsepower figures through turbocharging. The carbon fiber monocoque construction — a genuine racing-derived technology rather than a styling exercise — provides exceptional torsional rigidity at minimal weight.

Given Panoz's extremely limited production volume, each Avezzano receives individual attention during assembly that mass-production sports car manufacturers cannot replicate. This positions the car as a genuine alternative to low-volume European specialist manufacturers for buyers seeking authentic motorsport pedigree with American manufacturing provenance.

Buying Guide

Buying Guide & Brand History

Who Should Buy This

The Panoz Avezzano is for collectors and driving enthusiasts who prioritize genuine motorsport heritage and naturally-aspirated driving engagement over brand recognition or service network convenience. It suits buyers who already have specialist car ownership experience and appreciate extreme exclusivity. Buyers wanting daily usability, established dealer service, or maximum brand prestige recognition should consider established specialist manufacturers instead.

Brand & Model History

Panoz has competed at Le Mans and in American sports car racing since the 1990s, co-founding the American Le Mans Series. The Avezzano represents the company's continued commitment to translating endurance racing technology directly into limited-production road cars built in Hoschton, Georgia.

Frequently Asked Questions

What racing heritage does Panoz actually have?
Panoz has competed in Le Mans and American sports car racing (including the American Le Mans Series, which Panoz co-founded) since the 1990s, with multiple class podium finishes. This racing program directly informs the engineering philosophy and chassis technology used in road car production.
How does the Avezzano compare to European alternatives like a Lotus or Alpine?
The Avezzano offers comparable naturally-aspirated driving engagement with genuine American manufacturing and Le Mans-specific heritage. European alternatives generally have more established dealer networks and parts availability, while the Panoz offers greater exclusivity and a more direct connection to endurance racing technology.
Is the Panoz Avezzano street legal in all US states?
Panoz designs the Avezzano to meet federal motor vehicle safety standards for street-legal operation. Specific state registration requirements for low-volume manufacturers can vary, so verify registration feasibility in your state before ordering.
What is the typical wait time for a new Panoz Avezzano order?
Given extremely limited production volume, wait times can extend significantly beyond typical production car timelines. Contact Panoz directly for current production schedule and order queue information.
Where can the Avezzano be serviced?
Service is primarily handled at or coordinated through Panoz's Hoschton, Georgia facility given the limited dealer network. Buyers should factor potential service logistics into their ownership planning, particularly if located far from Georgia.
What warranty coverage comes with a new Avezzano?
Warranty terms should be confirmed directly with Panoz at time of purchase, as coverage specifics for low-volume specialist manufacturers can differ from mass-market warranty structures.
Does the Avezzano have any modern driver assistance technology?
Given the car's motorsport-focused engineering philosophy, driver assistance technology is generally minimal compared to mass-market vehicles, prioritizing direct driver engagement and feedback over automated intervention systems.
Is the Panoz Avezzano a good investment as a collector vehicle?
Extremely limited production volume and genuine motorsport heritage are factors that typically support long-term collector value, though specific investment outcomes cannot be guaranteed. Consult a specialist collector car advisor for investment-focused purchase decisions.
Does Panoz offer any factory racing or track day support programs?
Given the company's deep motorsport roots, Panoz has historically maintained connections to track day and racing communities. Specific current factory support programs should be verified directly with Panoz, as these offerings can change based on company resources and racing program priorities.
How does the carbon fiber monocoque affect insurance and repair costs?
Carbon fiber monocoque construction, while providing excellent strength-to-weight performance, generally results in higher insurance premiums and repair costs compared to traditional steel or aluminum chassis construction, since carbon fiber repair requires specialized facilities and cannot always be repaired using conventional body shop techniques.
