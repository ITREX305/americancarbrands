Overview

The 302 Black Label represents Steve Saleen's core belief: performance should be accessible. The 302 designation references the 302 cubic inch (5.0L) engine that Saleen has been tuning since the 1980s — the most refined version of that relationship is the Black Label, which takes the S650 Mustang and applies four decades of Saleen tuning knowledge to extract a naturally aspirated 425 hp.

Unlike the supercharged Super Snake or the extreme S7 LM, the 302 Black Label relies on careful breathing optimization, a Saleen cold air intake, high-flow exhaust, and an ECU recalibration that maintains factory drivability while adding meaningful power. The result is an engine that feels livelier throughout the rev range rather than simply hitting harder at the top end.

The Saleen aero package gives the 302 Black Label its visual identity: a Saleen-designed front fascia, side rockers with integrated splitters, and a rear spoiler tuned for stability at speed. Suspension uses Saleen-spec springs and shocks that lower the car 1 inch and improve cornering without brutally compromising ride quality on real roads.

Saleen serial numbers and registers every 302 Black Label — it enters the Saleen World Registry immediately, giving it the documentation and provenance that collector car buyers require. For $48,990, no other manufacturer offers this combination of genuine factory-performance pedigree and build documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Saleen 302 the same as a factory Mustang GT?
The 302 Black Label starts as a Mustang GT but receives Saleen's aero kit, suspension package, exhaust, ECU tune, and cold air intake. Power increases from 480 hp stock to 425 hp after Saleen's more aggressive but conservative tune of the air intake and ECU. The aero, suspension, and registry documentation differentiate it completely.
Does the Saleen 302 require premium fuel?
Yes — 93 octane premium is required for the Saleen ECU calibration. Using regular fuel will trigger knock retard and reduce power to below-stock levels. The Saleen tune is optimized for premium.
How does the 302 Black Label compare to a Shelby GT350?
The GT350 (removed from production) had a flat-plane V8 to 8,250 rpm — a more exotic engine. The 302 Black Label has the conventional cross-plane Coyote V8 tuned for accessible performance. The 302 is less extreme but more livable as a daily driver.
Can I order a specific color on the Saleen 302 Black Label?
Yes — Saleen builds on any available Mustang order code, meaning all Ford factory colors are available. Saleen also offers exclusive Saleen-spec colors not available from Ford directly. Contact Saleen at saleenautomotive.com for current color options.
What is included in Saleen World Registry registration?
Registry registration includes a serialized dash plaque, Saleen Authenticity Documentation, Certificate of Ownership, and a permanent entry in the Saleen World Registry database. This documentation is essential for resale value and collector market participation.
Is the Saleen 302 a reliable daily driver?
Yes — the Saleen 302 Black Label is built for daily drivability. The naturally aspirated tune doesn't add significant heat or mechanical stress. Most 302 owners report the car is as reliable as a standard Mustang GT, which has an excellent long-term reliability record.
Does the Saleen aero kit affect ground clearance?
The front fascia on the 302 Black Label is 0.5 inches lower than stock, which can cause scraping on steep driveways and aggressive parking lot approaches. Saleen owners typically learn their car's clearance limitations quickly.
What warranty covers the Saleen 302 Black Label?
Saleen provides a 3-year/36,000-mile limited warranty on Saleen-installed components. Ford's factory warranty covers the base Mustang components that Saleen has not modified.
