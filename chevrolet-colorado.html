The 2023 Colorado redesign helped GM reclaim mid-size truck market share. The 11.3-inch screen and upscale interior match full-size truck quality at a lower price point.

One Engine, Two Tunes

GM's choice to run one engine in two states simplifies the lineup while giving meaningful differentiation. The high-tune 2.7L is genuinely powerful for a mid-size truck.

Colorado uses a single 2.7L turbocharged four-cylinder in two tunes. The base WT gets 237 hp / 259 lb-ft; LT and above get 310 hp / 390 lb-ft — enough for a 0–60 of ~5.4 seconds and 7,700-lb towing.

05

Towing & Off-Road

ZR2 vs Raptor

ZR2 and Ranger Raptor are the two serious mid-size off-road trucks. ZR2's Multimatic DSSV shocks are arguably the finest dampers available on any production truck. Raptor wins on HP (405 vs 310). ZR2 wins on technical rock capability.

Max tow of 7,700 lb matches the Ranger at the top of the mid-size class. Payload of 1,690 lb is solid. The Trail Boss adds 2-inch lift and AT tires for light off-road; the ZR2 is a purpose-built trail truck.

06

ZR2 Detail

ZR2 Bison

An even more extreme ZR2 Bison package adds AEV-designed skid plates, underbody armor, and lifted suspension — making it the most capable production mid-size truck for rock crawling.

The ZR2 features Multimatic DSSV (Dynamic Spool Valve) position-sensitive dampers — race-proven technology shared with the Chevrolet Camaro Z/28. Front and rear electronic locking diffs, rock sliders, and underbody protection are all standard.

FAQ

What is the 2025 Colorado starting price?
WT starts at $32,595. LT ~$37,995. Trail Boss ~$42,995. ZR2 ~$53,995. Prices exclude destination (~$1,795).
How much can the Colorado tow?
LT and above with the high-output tune tow up to 7,700 lb. WT (low tune) is rated at 3,500 lb.
What makes the ZR2 special?
Multimatic DSSV position-sensitive shocks, front and rear electronic locking diffs, rock sliders, and all-terrain tires. It's the most capable mid-size off-road truck in the segment.
Colorado vs Ranger — which is better?
Both tow 7,700 lb. Ranger Raptor has 405 hp; ZR2 has better rock crawl capability. Colorado interior feels more premium. Ranger has longer track record. Both are excellent — choose by off-road vs daily driving priority.
Does the Colorado come in a diesel?
No — the US-market Colorado dropped its diesel after the 2nd generation. Only the 2.7L turbo four-cylinder is offered in two power states.
