Brand Overview

Steve Saleen began modifying Mustangs in 1983, earning an official Ford Special Vehicle Team relationship. Saleen cars are built on Ford Mustang platforms and certified by the NADA as distinct makes — a Saleen Mustang carries its own VIN designation and is recognized as a separate vehicle by insurance and title agencies. The S7, produced 2002–2008, was at one point the world's fastest production car with the 2005 Twin Turbo's 248 mph claimed top speed.

Current lineup: The S302 series remains the volume backbone — Yellow Label (street-oriented), White Label (track-capable), and Black Label (750 hp maximum). The S1 is a mid-engine sports car designed from the ground up. The S7 Le Mans is a spiritual revival of the legendary S7, targeting 1,000 hp in limited production. Warranty: Saleen vehicles carry Ford's base powertrain warranty plus Saleen's own limited vehicle warranty — verify at saleen.com before purchasing.

Recalls & Safety

Always check the current NHTSA recall database at nhtsa.gov/recalls using the VIN for your specific vehicle. The items below are representative of known actions — not a complete recall history.

Physical Recall2023
S302 — Ford Mustang Base Recall Applicability
Certain Saleen S302 vehicles built on 2022–2023 Mustang platforms may be subject to Ford-issued safety recalls on the base vehicle. Owners should check nhtsa.gov/recalls with the full VIN.
Physical Recall2021
S1 — Brake Caliper Bracket Inspection
Saleen issued a voluntary safety inspection for early S1 production vehicles regarding brake caliper bracket torque verification. Dealer inspection required. Contact Saleen directly for current status.
OTA Update2024
S302 — Ford SYNC Software Update
2023–2024 S302 vehicles with Ford SYNC 4 infotainment received an OTA software update addressing wireless Apple CarPlay stability. Applied automatically when connected to Wi-Fi.
Always Verify

Buying Tips 2026

Saleen S302 cars carry their own NADA-recognized VINs and are recognized as distinct makes — this matters for insurance, financing, and resale. The S302 Yellow Label is the most road-practical; the Black Label demands premium maintenance intervals given 750 hp stress on the powertrain. For used Saleens, verify full build documentation including Saleen build sheet and service history. S7 and S1 purchases should involve direct engagement with Saleen's factory team.

Before You Buy — Checklist

1. Verify the Saleen build sheet and certificate of authenticity for any used vehicle. 2. Confirm the specific S302 label (Yellow/White/Black) — each represents meaningfully different power and capability. 3. Check nhtsa.gov/recalls with the full VIN — Ford base recalls may apply. 4. For S1 or S7: arrange direct factory communication and verify current warranty terms. 5. Plan for premium fuel and more frequent service intervals vs. stock Mustang GT.

FAQ

Is a Saleen considered a separate car from a Ford Mustang?
Yes. Saleen vehicles built on Mustang platforms carry Saleen-issued VINs and are recognized by NADA, insurance companies, and DMVs as distinct makes. A Saleen Mustang is titled as a Saleen, not a Ford — similar to how Shelby vehicles are titled.
What is the Saleen S7?
The original Saleen S7 was an American mid-engine supercar produced 2002–2008. The 2005 S7 Twin Turbo, at 750 hp, reached a claimed 248 mph top speed, briefly making it one of the world's fastest production cars. The S7 Le Mans is a modern revival targeting 1,000 hp.
How reliable are Saleen Mustangs?
The underlying Ford Mustang GT platform has strong long-term reliability. Saleen's supercharger and tune add stress to the powertrain — Black Label 750 hp builds require premium maintenance. Yellow Label builds (470 hp) are closest to stock stress levels.
Does Ford warranty cover a Saleen Mustang?
The Ford powertrain warranty typically remains in effect on the base components when the vehicle is modified within Ford's guidelines. However, Saleen's supercharger modifications may affect warranty coverage on related components. Verify warranty status directly with Saleen and your Ford dealer before purchasing.
Who founded Saleen Automotive?
Steve Saleen, a professional racing driver, founded Saleen Autosport in 1983 in California. He began by modifying Ford Mustangs for street and track use, eventually gaining official recognition as a distinct vehicle manufacturer.
