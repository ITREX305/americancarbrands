Editorial Verdict

Which Should You Choose?

Both are iconic American performance cars at very different price points. Choose the Mustang if you want the lower entry price, the legendary 5.0L V8, or a 6-speed manual. Choose the Charger if you want a 4-door body for practicality, the HEMI's distinctive character, or the electric Daytona's 670hp EV performance. The Mustang is the purer sports car; the Charger is more practical for daily use.

Common Questions

In top-performance trims, the Charger SRT (3.6s 0-60) edges the Mustang Dark Horse (3.9s). However, the Mustang GT500 (now discontinued) was quicker than any V8 Charger in its day. The new electric Charger Daytona changes the equation with 670hp and sub-3-second capability.
Both have solid reliability records for performance cars. The Mustang's 5.0L Coyote V8 has an extensive reliability track record across many model years. The Charger's HEMI V8 is similarly proven but has known issues with spark plugs and lifters in high-mileage examples. Neither is unusually problematic.
The Mustang typically holds resale value better due to its higher sales volume and broader appeal. The Charger 4-door commands a strong used market premium due to its unique position as the only 4-door muscle car. Special editions of both (Hellcat, GT500) hold value exceptionally well.
