FAQ

What is Saleen?
Saleen Automotive, founded by Steve Saleen in 1983, builds performance Mustangs in Corona, California with individual S-number documentation.
What is an S-number?
Saleen's vehicle authentication number — similar to Shelby's CSM number. Documents each vehicle as a genuine Saleen build.
Where is it built?
Corona, California.
How do I buy a Saleen?
Through Saleen Automotive at saleen.com.
Is the Saleen street legal?
Yes — all Saleen S302 models are street-legal.
How does it compare to Shelby?
Both are authenticated Mustang-based performance vehicles. Shelby has longer history (1962) and wider recognition. Saleen offers comparable or greater power at similar price points.
