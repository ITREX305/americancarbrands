Overview

The Cybertruck entered production in late 2023 following years of delays from the originally announced 2021 target. What arrived was substantially different from the 2019 concept in specifications and pricing — a pattern that drew significant criticism from reservation holders who had been told to expect a $39,900–$69,900 range. The delivered product starts at $79,990.

What it delivers for that price is genuinely unlike anything else in the truck segment. The 301 stainless steel exoskeleton is not cosmetic — it eliminates body stamping infrastructure entirely, though it also means body repairs are fundamentally different from any other vehicle on the road. The adaptive air suspension adjusts ride height across four settings. The Vault bed, at 6 feet usable, includes a tonneau cover that's load-rated to support a person standing on it.

Performance numbers are real. The Cyberbeast's 2.6-second 0–60 time is verified by independent testing. The 11,000 lb tow rating is genuine — though towing heavily will compress real-world range to the 150–200 mile range depending on load and speed, which requires trip planning.

⚠ Towing + Range Planning

Heavy towing reduces Cybertruck range by 40–55% from rated figures. A 318-mile AWD Cybertruck towing 9,000 lb may deliver only 130–150 miles of range. Plan Supercharger stops every 120–130 miles in heavy towing scenarios. Tesla's navigation system provides towing-aware routing when a trailer is detected.

Towing Capabilities

The Cybertruck's tow ratings are competitive within the full-size truck segment, and exceptional among electric vehicles. At 11,000 lb for AWD and Cyberbeast trims, it surpasses the Ford F-150 Lightning (10,000 lb) and Rivian R1T (11,000 lb).

Recalls & Safety Actions

NHTSA Recall • 2024 • ~3,878 Vehicles
Accelerator Pedal Insert — May Become Dislodged
A rubber insert in the accelerator pedal assembly could become dislodged and lodge against the interior trim, potentially causing unintended acceleration. Remedy: inspection and replacement of accelerator pedal. Verify your VIN status at nhtsa.gov/recalls.
NHTSA Recall • 2024 • ~11,688 Vehicles
Wiper Blades — May Stop Operating
Front wiper blades could cease operation due to a software control issue, reducing driver visibility. Addressed via over-the-air software update. Confirm OTA update status in your Tesla app.
ⓘ Check Your VIN

Verify recall status for your specific vehicle at nhtsa.gov/recalls using your 17-character VIN. Many Tesla recalls are resolved via OTA software updates — confirm your vehicle's software version in the Tesla app.

★ Used Buying Advice

When buying a used Cybertruck, check the NHTSA recall history for that VIN — confirm all outstanding recalls have been resolved. Request the vehicle's service history. Inspect the stainless panels carefully: the material resists dents but surface oxidation and scratches are not repairable through conventional body shop processes. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the range of the 2026 Tesla Cybertruck?
EPA-rated range: 340 miles (RWD), 318 miles (AWD), 301 miles (Cyberbeast). Real-world highway range at 75 mph is approximately 260–270 miles for RWD. Cold weather reduces range 20–40%. Towing reduces range significantly — budget 40–55% reduction when towing near rated capacity.
How much does the Tesla Cybertruck cost in 2026?
The 2026 Cybertruck starts at $79,990 (RWD), $89,990 (AWD), and $99,990 (Cyberbeast). Add destination charges (~$1,390). The Cybertruck does not qualify for the federal EV tax credit at any trim level — all three exceed the $80,000 MSRP cap for the credit. Verify current pricing at tesla.com.
How much can the Cybertruck tow?
The AWD and Cyberbeast trims are rated at 11,000 lb maximum tow capacity. The RWD is rated at 7,500 lb. At maximum tow loads, real-world range is approximately 130–160 miles. Always use Tesla's towing-aware navigation routing for range planning.
Has the Cybertruck been recalled?
Yes. Two notable 2024 recalls: the accelerator pedal insert issue (~3,878 vehicles, physical repair required) and a wiper software issue (~11,688 vehicles, resolved via OTA). Check your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for current status.
Does the Cybertruck qualify for the EV tax credit?
No. All three Cybertruck trims start above $80,000 MSRP, which is the federal cap for truck/SUV EV tax credit eligibility. None of the current Cybertruck variants qualify for the Section 30D clean vehicle credit. Verify current tax credit rules at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxcenter.do.
How does the Cybertruck compare to the F-150 Lightning?
The F-150 Lightning starts at ~$49,995 — significantly less than the Cybertruck's $79,990 entry. The Lightning tows 10,000 lb vs 11,000 lb for the Cybertruck AWD. The Lightning's Pro and XLT trims qualify for the EV tax credit. The Cybertruck offers higher performance (especially Cyberbeast), a unique stainless body, and air suspension. For value, the Lightning wins at base trims. For performance and capability ceiling, Cybertruck has the advantage.
