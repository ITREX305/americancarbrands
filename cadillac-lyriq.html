Range & Charging

EPA-rated range is tested at moderate speed/temp. Real-world at 70 mph is approximately 244 miles. Cold weather (32°F) reduces range ~22%. Towing near max capacity reduces range 45–55%. Always plan charging stops when towing long distances.

190 kW Charging

At 190 kW, the LYRIQ charges faster than the BMW iX (195 kW — essentially tied), Audi e-tron GT (270 kW — faster), and Genesis GV80 Coupe (240 kW). Competitive for the luxury EV class.

The RWD LYRIQ achieves 314 miles EPA. DC fast charging at 190 kW adds approximately 80 miles in 10 minutes. NACS connector is standard — Tesla Supercharger access included.

05

Interior & Tech

Super Cruise at $65K

At $64,990, the AWD LYRIQ is the most affordable Cadillac to offer Super Cruise — making hands-free highway driving accessible below the $70K threshold for the first time.

The 33-inch AKG-equipped diagonal screen is standard across all LYRIQ models. Super Cruise hands-free driving on 400,000+ mapped road miles is available on AWD and standard on V Sport.

06

LYRIQ-V Sport

V Sport vs Model Y Performance

LYRIQ-V Sport: 557 hp, $74,990, Super Cruise, Cadillac interior. Model Y Performance: 507 hp, $52,990, stronger resale. LYRIQ wins on luxury and hands-free coverage. Model Y wins on value and range (303 vs 287 mi V Sport).

The LYRIQ-V Sport brings 557 hp, Brembo brakes, performance adaptive suspension, and Super Cruise as standard. 0–60 in 3.9 seconds. This is Cadillac's V performance sub-brand applied to its first electric vehicle.

FAQ

What is the 2026 LYRIQ starting price?
Luxury RWD starts at $59,990. AWD at $64,990. V Sport at $74,990. Prices exclude destination (~$1,295).
How far does the LYRIQ go on a charge?
RWD: 314 miles EPA. AWD: 307 miles. V Sport AWD: 287 miles.
Does the LYRIQ have NACS charging?
Yes — NACS standard on all LYRIQ models. Tesla Supercharger access via Cadillac app.
What is the LYRIQ-V Sport?
Cadillac's V performance brand applied to the LYRIQ — 557 hp, 3.9-second 0–60, Brembo brakes, Super Cruise standard.
LYRIQ vs Tesla Model Y — which is better?
LYRIQ wins: luxury interior, Super Cruise 400K+ mi, Cadillac brand. Model Y wins: lower price ($52K vs $60K), stronger resale, more Supercharger locations. Clear luxury vs value split.
