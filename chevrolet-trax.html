Overview

The 2026 Chevrolet Trax is the most affordable new SUV in Chevrolet's lineup and one of the best-value vehicles in America today. Completely redesigned for 2024, the third-generation Trax arrived with a longer wheelbase, a dramatically wider interior, and a bold exterior design that looks far more expensive than its $21,600 starting price suggests. Chevrolet invested significantly in this redesign to ensure the Trax could compete with Korean rivals on value while retaining the brand trust that has kept it a consistent top seller in the subcompact segment.

Under the hood sits a turbocharged 1.2-liter three-cylinder engine producing 137 horsepower and 162 lb-ft of torque, paired with a 6-speed automatic transmission. The engine is modest in outright power but delivers smooth, responsive performance in the real-world city and suburban driving conditions where most Trax owners spend their time. Fuel economy of 28 mpg city and 33 mpg highway is competitive with segment leaders and translates to approximately $1,900 in annual fuel costs at current prices.

The interior is the Trax's biggest surprise. The redesigned cabin is genuinely spacious for the price, with 11.3 additional inches of rear legroom compared to its predecessor. A standard 11.0-inch infotainment display with wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto is class-competitive at this price point. An 8.0-inch digital instrument cluster is standard across most trims, giving the Trax a premium feel that competitors at this price cannot replicate. USB-C ports front and rear eliminate the frustrating adapter situation of previous generations.

Safety technology is comprehensive at every trim level: automatic emergency braking, lane keep assist, forward collision warning, and rear park assist are all standard. The Trax competes directly with the Hyundai Venue, Kia Soul, Nissan Kicks, and Honda HR-V — all capable products that the Trax matches or exceeds on interior space, standard technology features, and overall value per dollar. For first-time buyers, young families, and urban commuters on a budget, no competitor delivers more for less.

Costs & Comparison

Ownership Costs & Value

True Cost of Ownership

Purchase price is only one factor. Insurance, fuel, maintenance, and depreciation significantly affect 5-year total cost. Research all factors before committing.

Total cost of ownership on the Trax is exceptionally low. At $21,600 base, financing at 7% over 60 months costs approximately $400/month. Insurance runs $100-160/month comprehensive for most drivers. Fuel at 15,000 miles/year on regular 87 octane (28/33 mpg) costs approximately $1,900/year. Maintenance follows Chevrolet's Oil Life Monitoring system — expect oil changes every 7,500-10,000 miles on full synthetic at $70-90/change. The 5-year/60,000-mile powertrain warranty eliminates major engine and transmission repair risk. Total 5-year ownership cost is estimated at $28,000-$33,000 including depreciation.

Buying Guide

Performance & Powertrain Analysis

The 1.2L turbocharged three-cylinder delivers its 137 hp and 162 lb-ft of torque in a smooth, linear fashion suited to its mission. City acceleration is adequate, highway merging requires planning, and the 6-speed automatic manages the modest power reserves without hunting or strain. The Trax is not and should not be evaluated as a performance vehicle — it is a practical urban transportation solution, and in that context the powertrain is well-matched. FWD is standard on LS, LT, and RS trims; the ACTIV adds AWD via a mechanical rear-mounted system.

Interior, Technology & Comfort

The redesigned 2024+ generation interior is a genuine leap over the previous Trax. The swept dashboard with 11-inch touchscreen feels modern and functional. Rear seat legroom at 40.3 inches is remarkable in a vehicle that is only 174 inches long overall — a packaging achievement that outpoints more expensive competitors. Cargo space at 25.3 cubic feet behind the rear seats is competitive. The Chevrolet Infotainment 3 system with wireless CarPlay and Android Auto is reliable and responsive. The 8-inch digital cluster adds to the premium feel without adding cost.

Who Should Buy the Trax?

The Trax is best suited for first-time car buyers who need modern safety technology on a tight budget, urban commuters who need parking-friendly dimensions with genuine interior space, small families who want a new vehicle without heavy monthly payments, and anyone who drives primarily in city and suburban environments. It is not the right choice for buyers who regularly take highway road trips demanding refinement, anyone who needs AWD without upgrading to ACTIV, or buyers who prioritize premium cabin materials over functionality.

Frequently Asked Questions

The following addresses the most common questions buyers have about the 2026 Chevrolet Trax. Answers reflect published data and independent analysis — verify current information with your Chevrolet dealer.

What is the 2026 Chevrolet Trax starting price?

The 2026 Chevrolet Trax starts at $21,600 for the base LS trim before destination charges (approximately $1,195) and taxes. The LT runs $24,300, the sporty RS $27,200, and the adventure-focused ACTIV (with AWD) $28,100. Verify current pricing and available incentives at your local Chevrolet dealer.

Does the 2026 Trax have AWD?

AWD is available exclusively on the ACTIV trim starting at $28,100. All other Trax trims — LS, LT, and RS — are front-wheel drive only. If AWD is a priority for year-round driving in snow country, the ACTIV is the required trim, or consider stepping up to the Trailblazer which offers AWD on more trim levels.

How does the Trax compare to the Hyundai Venue?

The Venue starts at approximately $19,850, slightly less than the Trax. However, the Venue is notably smaller inside — particularly rear seat space. The Trax offers an 11-inch standard touchscreen versus the Venue's smaller display. The Trax is a better choice for buyers who need rear seat space; the Venue for buyers who want the smallest possible footprint at the lowest price.

Is the 2026 Trax reliable?

The third-generation Trax (2024+) has accumulated positive early owner reports. The 1.2L turbocharged engine is shared with other GM products and has a good reliability record. J.D. Power's initial quality data for the current generation is favorable. Note that the older second-generation Trax (2013-2022) had more mixed reliability scores — verify you're looking at current-gen data when researching.

What fuel does the Trax require?

The 2026 Trax requires regular 87 octane unleaded fuel — no premium required. This keeps annual fuel costs low: at 15,000 miles per year and $3.50/gallon, you'll spend approximately $1,900 on fuel. This is a meaningful operating cost advantage over vehicles requiring premium fuel.

Does the 2026 Trax have Android Auto?

Yes — wireless Android Auto is standard on all 2026 Trax trims. The system connects to your Android phone without a cable, projecting navigation, music, and messaging apps onto the 11-inch touchscreen. Wireless Apple CarPlay is also standard. This level of wireless connectivity at the LS base price point is uncommon in the segment.

What is the Trax's towing capacity?

The Chevrolet Trax is not rated for towing — Chevrolet does not publish a tow rating for this model. It is designed as a people-mover, not a hauler. For towing capability, step up to the Equinox (1,500 lb) or Traverse (5,000 lb) in the Chevrolet lineup.

How does the Trax ACTIV compare to a standard Trax?

The ACTIV adds AWD, a 1-inch suspension lift, 17-inch all-terrain tires, orange exterior accents, and skid plates. It starts at $28,100 — $7,700 more than the base LS. The ACTIV is the only Trax with AWD and the only one designed for mild off-road or adverse weather use. For most urban buyers, the FWD LS or LT delivers better value. For buyers in snowy climates or who occasionally go off-road, the ACTIV justifies the premium.
