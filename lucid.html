02

Brand Overview

Dreaming Forward. Founded 2007 in Newark, California as Atieva — an EV battery and technology company. Renamed Lucid Motors in 2016. Lucid produces the Air luxury sedan, which holds the US EPA record for production EV range at 516 miles (Air Grand Touring Performance). The company's AMP-1 factory in Casa Grande, Arizona began production in 2021.

Parent / Ownership: Independent public company (NASDAQ: LCID). Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) holds approximately 60%+ majority stake and has provided multiple rounds of capital. HQ: Newark, California. Manufacturing: Casa Grande, Arizona (AMP-1).

The Lucid Air Sapphire tri-motor variant produces 1,234 hp — making it one of the most powerful production sedans ever built. 0-60 in 1.89 seconds. The Gravity SUV (2025+) uses similar technology in a 7-passenger configuration with estimated ~450 miles EPA. Lucid's technology focus includes its proprietary Wunderbox charging system, capable of handling up to 924 miles per hour of range when connected to a 350 kW charger.

Reliability

Lucid Air reliability data is limited by low production volume. Early owner reports (2022–2023) are generally positive on powertrain and range delivery. Initial production quality improved from 2022 to 2024 builds. Lucid's service network is limited — approximately 30+ service centers concentrated in major metros — which is the primary ownership concern outside California and key urban areas.

Buying Tips 2026

The Lucid Air Pure at $69,900 makes the brand more accessible but service proximity remains the critical factor — verify a Lucid service center is within reasonable distance before committing. The Air Grand Touring at $138,000 is the sweet spot: excellent range, real-world performance, and sufficient production volume for reasonable wait times. The Gravity SUV is a first-year model — consider waiting for 2026/2027 builds if reliability data matters. Two-year complimentary Electrify America charging is a genuine value add on some trims.

FAQ

What is the range of the Lucid Air?
The Lucid Air holds the US EPA record for production vehicle range. The Air Grand Touring Performance is rated at 516 miles EPA — the longest of any production car sold in the US. The Air Pure (entry level) is rated at 410 miles. The Air Sapphire (performance tri-motor) is rated at 420 miles. Verify current EPA ratings at fueleconomy.gov.
Who owns Lucid Motors?
Lucid is a public company (NASDAQ: LCID). Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) holds approximately 60%+ of outstanding shares and has been the primary source of capital since 2018. The PIF has committed to purchasing up to 100,000 Lucid vehicles for use in Saudi Arabia. Other institutional investors hold the remaining shares.
How does the Lucid Air compare to Tesla Model S?
The Lucid Air has more range (up to 516 mi vs. 405 mi for Model S Long Range), more efficient powertrain technology (4.65 miles/kWh vs. ~3.5 for Model S), and a more premium interior by most reviewer assessments. The Tesla Model S has the advantage of native Supercharger access, far larger service network, and more proven long-term reliability data. The Air starts from $69,900 vs. $74,990 for Model S.
What is the Lucid Gravity?
The Lucid Gravity is Lucid's 7-passenger electric SUV, launched in 2025. Starting at ~$94,900 for the Grand Touring trim. Estimated EPA range of ~440 miles. It seats up to 7 passengers with a configuration similar to a Volvo XC90 or Audi Q7. It is Lucid's second production vehicle and uses the same technology base as the Air.
Is Lucid financially stable?
Lucid is operating at a significant loss while scaling production — a common pattern for early-stage EV manufacturers. Revenue was $595M in FY2023. Saudi Arabia's PIF majority ownership provides substantial capital backing and committed vehicle purchase orders. Path to profitability requires substantially higher production volume. Monitor quarterly earnings at lucidmotors.com/investors.
