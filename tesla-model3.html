Overview

The Tesla Model 3 is a compact electric sedan and the most important vehicle in Tesla's history — the car that brought EVs to mass market. The 2024 Highland redesign brought a new front fascia, revised rear, dramatically improved interior quality, and better range. It remains one of the most efficient and capable electric sedans at any price. The Model 3 is built at Gigafactory Fremont (California) and Gigafactory Shanghai (China). US buyers receive Fremont-built vehicles.

FAQ

What is the Tesla Model 3 range in 2026?
The Model 3 RWD is rated at 272 miles EPA. The Long Range AWD reaches 358 miles EPA — best in class for compact EVs under $50,000. The Performance AWD is rated at 315 miles. All figures are from EPA standardized testing; real-world highway range is typically 10–15% lower.
Does the Model 3 qualify for the federal EV tax credit?
Some Model 3 configurations may qualify for the $7,500 federal EV tax credit under the IRA, subject to MSRP caps ($55,000 for sedans), buyer income limits (~$150K single / $300K joint), and battery/assembly sourcing requirements. Verify current eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxcenter.do — rules can change.
What is the difference between the Model 3 Highland and older Model 3?
The Highland refresh (launched 2024) brought a redesigned exterior with full-width LED rear light bar, dramatically improved interior with better materials and ambient lighting, updated 15.4" touchscreen, and improved range. If you're considering a used Model 3, 2024+ Highland builds are meaningfully different in quality from 2021–2023 builds.
What is the Model 3's charging speed?
The Model 3 Long Range AWD charges at up to 250 kW at a V3 Supercharger — reaching 10–80% in approximately 20–25 minutes. The base RWD model charges at up to 170 kW. Home Level 2 charging adds approximately 30–35 miles of range per hour.
Is the Tesla Model 3 reliable?
The Model 3's powertrain (motor, battery, inverter) has strong long-term reliability — fewer failure modes than ICE vehicles. Build quality has historically been below average per Consumer Reports, though 2024+ Highland builds show significant improvement. Battery degradation is modest — ~10% capacity loss over 150,000 miles in typical conditions.
