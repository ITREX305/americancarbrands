Brand Overview

Professional Grade. Founded 1911, the same year as Chevrolet, GMC is General Motors' premium-positioned truck and SUV brand. It shares platforms and powertrains with Chevrolet but commands higher transaction prices through premium trim levels — Denali (luxury), AT4/AT4X (off-road) — and distinct exterior styling.

Parent / Ownership: General Motors (NYSE: GM). HQ: Detroit, Michigan. Founded: 1911. Key differentiator: the HUMMER EV — a purpose-built, ultra-performance electric supertruck at 1,000 hp with Crab Walk mode (all-four-wheel steering diagonal movement) and available 0-60 in 3.0 seconds (WTF Edition).

GMC's Denali trim philosophy — higher content, unique exterior, and premium pricing — has been successful enough that Denali accounts for approximately 30–40% of GMC truck sales. The Sierra Denali Ultimate is GMC's most premium pickup configuration.

Reliability

GMC reliability is broadly comparable to sister brand Chevrolet — both use the same platforms and powertrains. The Sierra and Yukon long-term powertrain reliability is strong. The HUMMER EV is too new for long-term data. GM's Infotainment 3 system has been a documented concern across the lineup, though software updates have improved it substantially.

Buying Tips 2026

GMC prices run higher than equivalent Chevrolet for the same underlying platform — the Denali premium is real but so is the price gap. If budget is the priority, compare Sierra vs. Silverado same trim carefully. HUMMER EV: waitlists have eased, making negotiation possible. Canyon AT4X competes with Ford Bronco Sport Badlands — compare off-road specs carefully. Super Cruise on Yukon Denali Ultimate requires subscription after trial period (~$25/month).

08

FAQ

What is the GMC HUMMER EV Crab Walk?
Crab Walk is a GMC HUMMER EV-exclusive feature that uses four-wheel steering to move the vehicle diagonally — all four wheels turn in the same direction simultaneously. This allows the vehicle to navigate tight off-road situations at low speeds. It requires special terrain settings and does not work at highway speeds.
What is the difference between GMC and Chevrolet?
GMC and Chevrolet are both General Motors brands. They share the same platforms, engines, and manufacturing facilities, but GMC is positioned as the premium truck/SUV brand. GMC offers exclusive trim levels (Denali, AT4X) and distinct exterior styling. GMC does not sell sedans or subcompact vehicles — it is trucks and SUVs only. Expect to pay 10–15% more for equivalent GMC vs. Chevrolet.
What is GMC Sierra EV?
The GMC Sierra EV is the all-electric version of the Sierra pickup, sharing the Ultium platform with the Silverado EV and HUMMER EV. The Denali Edition 1 starts at ~$107,000 and offers up to 400 miles of estimated range. Lower Pro trim starts at ~$75,000. NACS charging standard.
Does GMC make a 3-row SUV?
Yes — the GMC Yukon (standard wheelbase) and Yukon XL (extended wheelbase) offer 8-passenger seating. The Acadia also offers optional 3-row seating. The Yukon Denali and Denali Ultimate are among the most premium American 3-row SUVs available.
What is the GMC HUMMER EV's range?
The HUMMER EV pickup in EV3X and WTF editions is rated at 329 miles EPA. The SUV variant is rated at 314 miles EPA. These are significant compromises relative to lighter EVs due to the vehicle's extreme weight (~9,000+ lbs). Expect real-world highway range of 250–280 miles.
