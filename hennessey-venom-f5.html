1,817-hp Fury Engine

The "Fury" is a Hennessey-developed 6.6L twin-turbo V8 producing 1,817 hp — the most powerful production car engine ever built in the United States.

Most Powerful American Car

1,817 hp exceeds the Bugatti Chiron Super Sport (1,578 hp) and SSC Tuatara (1,750 hp) — the highest output from any US production vehicle.
