Buying Tips 2026

Verify current pricing and incentives at the manufacturer website. Get a pre-purchase inspection from a certified independent mechanic for used vehicles. Check all open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchasing.

Before You Buy — Checklist

1. Verify current pricing at manufacturer website (prices change frequently). 2. Check all open NHTSA recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls using the VIN. 3. Confirm any EV/PHEV tax credit eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxcenter.do. 4. Get a pre-purchase inspection from an independent certified shop for used vehicles. 5. Compare Consumer Reports and J.D. Power data for your specific model year.

FAQ

Where can I verify current Mack Trucks pricing?
Always verify current pricing at the official manufacturer website before visiting a dealer. Prices change frequently and exclude destination charges, taxes, and fees.
How do I check for open recalls on a Mack Trucks vehicle?
Enter your VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls for a free official recall check. Always do this before purchasing a used vehicle.
Where can I find Mack Trucks reliability data?
Check Consumer Reports (consumerreports.org) and J.D. Power (jdpower.com) for current reliability ratings. Verify data for your specific model year before purchasing.
