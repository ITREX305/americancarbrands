Ford Maverick vs Chevy Trax — 2026 Head-to-Head

The Ford Maverick and Chevrolet Trax are the two most interesting American vehicles under $30,000 in 2026. They solve different problems — which makes the comparison more nuanced than a typical head-to-head.

ⓘ The Short Answer

Buy the Trax if: you want the lowest possible price, you need a small urban vehicle, and you never need to haul large items or tow. Buy the Maverick if: you can benefit from a truck bed, you drive significant highway miles, and you want the lowest possible fuel costs. The Maverick costs $6,445 more — but the 5-year fuel savings close most of that gap.

Trax LS costs $6,445 less than the Maverick Hybrid at base MSRP. Maverick saves ~$2,555 in fuel over 5 years. Net advantage to Trax: ~$3,890 over 5 years — before accounting for the Maverick's truck bed utility. If you use the bed even occasionally, the premium for the Maverick is justified.

FAQ
Is the Ford Maverick better than the Chevy Trax?
They solve different problems. The Maverick is better if you need a truck bed and want low fuel costs. The Trax is better if you want the lowest price and maximum interior cargo space in a small package. Neither is objectively better.
Which is more reliable — Maverick or Trax?
Both are relatively new platforms without extensive long-term reliability data. Early Consumer Reports data gives both above-average marks. The Maverick's hybrid system has more complexity than the Trax's turbocharged 1.2L — though Ford's hybrid systems (derived from the Escape/Fusion) have a strong track record.
Can you tow with a Chevy Trax?
The Trax is rated for 1,000 lb towing. The Maverick Hybrid is rated 2,000 lb (EcoBoost 2.0L AWD: 4,000 lb). Neither competes with a traditional truck for towing — but the Maverick has a meaningful advantage if you need to haul a small trailer or jet ski.
