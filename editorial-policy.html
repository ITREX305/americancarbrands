Editorial Policy

AmericanCarBrands.com is committed to accuracy, transparency, and independence in all editorial content. This page describes our standards, processes, and policies.

Editorial Independence

All content on AmericanCarBrands.com is produced by our editorial team without influence from advertisers, manufacturers, dealers, or affiliate partners. We are not paid to produce favorable coverage of any brand, model, or product. Our affiliate program and advertising relationships have no influence on editorial content, ratings, or recommendations.

Information Sources

Our content draws from the following primary sources:

  • Official manufacturer media releases and specification sheets
  • NHTSA regulatory filings, recall databases, and safety ratings
  • EPA fueleconomy.gov fuel economy and emissions data
  • IIHS crash safety ratings and research
  • Publicly filed SEC disclosures for publicly traded automakers
  • Owner and operator community reports verified against multiple independent sources

Accuracy Standards

Pricing information reflects MSRP as reported by manufacturer media at the time of publication. Prices change frequently and should always be verified at the manufacturer's official website. Specifications are sourced from manufacturer media and regulatory filings; discrepancies or updates should be verified with the manufacturer.

Correction Policy

We are committed to correcting factual errors promptly. If you believe any information on this Site is factually incorrect, please contact us with specific details including the URL of the page, the incorrect information, and your source for the correct information. We will review all correction requests and issue corrections to verified factual errors as quickly as possible.

Coverage Scope

AmericanCarBrands.com covers American car brands — defined as brands with significant US manufacturing, design, engineering, or operational heritage. This includes traditional Big Three brands, independent performance specialists, EV startups, and commercial vehicle manufacturers. We do not cover foreign brands whose primary operations, manufacturing, and identity are outside the United States.

No Sponsored Content

AmericanCarBrands.com does not publish sponsored content, native advertising, or manufacturer-produced content presented as independent editorial. All content on this Site is produced by our editorial team. Advertising placements (Google AdSense) are clearly separated from editorial content.
