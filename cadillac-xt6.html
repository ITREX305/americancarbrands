Overview

The 2026 Cadillac XT6 fills the 3-row slot between Buick Enclave and Cadillac Escalade. It is the most affordable Cadillac with 3-row seating — a meaningful entry point for buyers who want the Cadillac brand without Escalade pricing.

Platform Leverage

XT6 shares the GM C1 platform with Buick Enclave and GMC Acadia. Cadillac's version gets the most premium interior treatment and the only Super Cruise availability in this platform group.

No 4-Cyl Tax

The XT6's commitment to V6-across-all-trims means buyers aren't navigating around an underpowered entry model. Every XT6 — $53K through $64K — has the same 310-hp engine.

Unlike the XT5 which offers a 2.0L turbo base, the XT6 comes standard with the 3.6L V6 (310 hp) on every single trim. At $53,490, every XT6 buyer gets the full V6 experience — no entry-level compromise.

05

3-Row Interior

XT6 vs Enclave

Same GM C1 platform. Enclave seats 8, has 97.6 cu ft max cargo, starts $3,500 cheaper. XT6 has Cadillac badge, 33-inch screen, V-series adjacent styling, and available Super Cruise. Choose Enclave for space; XT6 for prestige.

Three-row seating for 6 (captains) or 7 (bench in second row). Third-row access is via folding second-row seats. Cargo with all rows folded reaches 78.7 cubic feet — less than the Buick Enclave on the same platform (97.6 cu ft), but with the Cadillac interior step-up.

06

XT6 Value Position

Super Cruise Gap

XT6 with Super Cruise covers 400,000+ mapped US/Canada miles. Lincoln Aviator with BlueCruise covers 130,000 miles. For hands-free highway driving, the XT6's coverage is meaningfully broader.

At $59,490 for the Luxury trim, the XT6 competes with the Lincoln Aviator ($57,490), BMW X5 ($68K), and Audi Q7 ($63K). XT6 wins on standard features and Super Cruise coverage. Aviator wins on PHEV option. BMW and Audi win on dynamics.

FAQ

What is the 2026 XT6 starting price?
Premium Luxury starts at $53,490. Luxury ~$59,490. Sport ~$63,990. Prices exclude destination (~$1,295).
XT6 vs Buick Enclave?
Same platform. Enclave: more cargo (97.6 vs 78.7 cu ft), seats 8, starts $3,500 cheaper. XT6: Cadillac badge, 33-inch screen standard, Super Cruise available. Space vs prestige — your call.
Does the XT6 have Super Cruise?
Yes — available on Luxury and Sport, and as an option on Premium Luxury.
XT6 vs Lincoln Aviator?
Aviator: $57,490 starting, has PHEV option (494 hp, 33-mi EV), BlueCruise 130K mi. XT6: $53,490 base, Super Cruise 400K mi, no PHEV. Choose Aviator for electrification; XT6 for broader hands-free coverage.
Does the XT6 have a third row?
Yes — 3-row seating standard for 6 (captain's) or 7 (bench 2nd row).
Is the a good value in 2026?
The delivers competitive value in its segment. We recommend cross-shopping alternatives and checking current incentive programs before purchasing. Verify all current pricing and incentives with your local Cadillac dealer.
