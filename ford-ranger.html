Overview

The 2025 Ford Ranger is the most powerful and capable Ranger ever sold in North America. Ford returned the nameplate in 2019 and redesigned it for 2024 with a longer wheelbase, more power, and a genuine factory Raptor.

The Ranger competes with the Chevrolet Colorado, Toyota Tacoma, and GMC Canyon. It leads the segment in tow capacity and features the most capable Raptor variant.

EPA highway rating measured at 55 mph. Real-world highway at 70 mph is typically 25–27 mpg. Towing near max capacity reduces highway fuel economy 30–40% — budget accordingly for trailer trips.

The 2025 Ranger uses a revised 2.3L EcoBoost producing 270 hp — up from 203 hp in the prior generation. The 10-speed automatic is smooth and well-matched.

Fuel economy reaches 22 city / 27 hwy mpg in 4x2 configuration — among the best in the mid-size segment. The Raptor's 3.0L V6 trades efficiency for 405 hp and 430 lb-ft.

Towing & Payload

Max tow of 7,500 lb (non-Raptor) is class-competitive. Payload tops at 1,805 lb. A 5-foot or 6-foot bed is available depending on the SuperCab or SuperCrew configuration.

Available Pro Power Onboard on Lariat+ provides up to 2.0 kW of exportable power — useful for job sites and camping.

06

Raptor Off-Road

The Ranger Raptor brings Fox Live Valve electronically controlled dampers, 33-inch BFGoodrich KO2 all-terrain tires, a Watt's linkage rear suspension, and 7 terrain modes including Baja and Rock Crawl.

Ground clearance reaches 12.1 inches — more than the Bronco base. The Raptor is purpose-built for high-speed desert running as well as technical rock trails.

FAQ

What is the 2025 Ford Ranger starting price?
The XL starts at $34,995. XLT around $40,495, Lariat around $46,995, and the Raptor at $56,995. Prices exclude destination (~$1,695).
How much can the Ranger tow?
Non-Raptor Ranger tows up to 7,500 lb — best in the mid-size class. The Raptor is not rated for towing.
Is the Ranger Raptor new for 2025?
The Ranger Raptor launched in North America for 2024 with a 405-hp 3.0L EcoBoost V6 and Fox Live Valve suspension. The 2025 adds new color options but carries over mechanically.
How does the Ranger compare to the Chevy Colorado?
Both tow 7,500 lb. The Ranger Raptor is more powerful than the Colorado ZR2; the Colorado offers a diesel option. Both have excellent off-road trim variants.
What's the difference between SuperCab and SuperCrew?
SuperCrew has full-size rear doors and a 5-ft bed. SuperCab has smaller rear doors and a 6-ft bed. Most buyers choose SuperCrew for rear seat comfort.
