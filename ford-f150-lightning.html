Range & Efficiency

EPA-rated range is tested at moderate speed/temp. Real-world at 70 mph is approximately 249 miles. Cold weather (32°F) reduces range ~25%. Towing near max capacity reduces range 47–57%. Always plan charging stops when towing long distances.

The standard-range (87.7-kWh) Lightning achieves up to 230 miles EPA. The extended-range (131-kWh) battery boosts range to 320 miles — enough for most weekend trips without stopping.

Regenerative braking is adjustable via One-Pedal Drive. The Lightning charges on any Level 2 AC outlet (up to 19.2 kW onboard charger) and DC fast charging at up to 150 kW on the extended-range pack.

05

Towing & Payload

Extended-range Lariat and Platinum tow up to 9,100 lb — competitive with many gas half-tons. Standard-range trims are rated at up to 7,700 lb.

Payload reaches 2,235 lb on extended-range models. The hydraulic lowering tailgate and onboard scales (available) simplify loading.

06

Charging & Tech

The Lightning ships with SYNC 4A and a 15.5-inch touchscreen on Lariat+. Over-the-air updates keep software current. BlueCruise hands-free highway driving is available.

NACS (Tesla-style) connector is standard, giving access to Tesla Superchargers plus Ford's BlueOval Charging Network of 100,000+ public charge points.

FAQ

What is the 2025 F-150 Lightning starting price?
The base Pro trim starts at $52,995. The extended-range Lariat starts around $68,995 and the Platinum at $84,995. All prices exclude destination (~$1,995).
How far can the F-150 Lightning tow?
Extended-range models tow up to 9,100 lb. Standard-range trims are rated to 7,700 lb. Towing significantly reduces range — budget 50–60% of EPA range when towing near max.
Does the F-150 Lightning have NACS?
Yes. All 2025 Lightning models ship with NACS connectors, giving plug-and-play access to Tesla Superchargers and the Ford BlueOval network via the FordPass app.
Can the Lightning power my home?
Yes — with Ford Intelligent Backup Power and a compatible home energy gateway, extended-range Lariat/Platinum can export up to 9.6 kW to keep essential circuits running during outages.
How long to charge the F-150 Lightning?
On a 48A Level 2 home charger, extended-range charges in ~10–11 hrs. DC fast charging at 150 kW adds ~50 miles per 10 minutes.
What EV tax credits apply?

Electric vehicles assembled in North America may qualify for up to $7,500 in federal EV tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Income limits and MSRP caps apply. Verify eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxevb.shtml before purchasing.
