Hypercar Performance

1,300+ hp from a twin-turbo V8 in a mid-engine layout. Estimated 0-60 under 2.8 seconds. The S7 Le Mans positions itself alongside the Bugatti Veyron and SSC Tuatara as an American hypercar.

American Hypercar

The S7 Le Mans is one of very few production vehicles from any country to claim 1,300+ hp with a Le Mans racing heritage.

05

Carbon Fiber Construction

Full carbon fiber body construction reduces weight dramatically vs steel. The S7 uses bespoke carbon panels throughout.

06

Le Mans Heritage

The original Saleen S7 raced at Le Mans in the early 2000s. The S7 Le Mans revives that heritage with modern hypercar performance.

Racing Pedigree

Saleen raced the original S7 in FIA GT racing including Le Mans — one of the few American production car manufacturers to compete at that level.
