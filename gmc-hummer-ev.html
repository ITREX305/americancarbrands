What Changed · March 2026

Hummer EV 2025–2026 Updates

🔋 3X Pickup: 381 mi EPA Range
Highest range in the Hummer EV lineup. Real-world highway is typically 320–340 mi unloaded at 65–70 mph. Towing at near-max capacity (7,500 lbs) estimated to cut range 50–60%.
🦀 Crab Walk — Standard on 3X
Low-speed diagonal movement via 4-wheel steering. Genuine off-road utility, not a gimmick. Operates below ~8 mph. Not available on 2X trim.
⛰️ Extract Mode (3X)
6-inch air suspension lift for off-road clearance. Combined with 18-camera UltraVision system makes this one of the most capable production off-roaders available.
⚠ No Federal EV Tax Credit
MSRP exceeds the $80,000 IRS vehicle price cap for the Section 30D clean vehicle credit. Verify current tax code changes at irs.gov ↗.
🏭 Built at Factory ZERO, Detroit MI
GM's dedicated EV plant. Production capacity growing through 2026. Dealer allocation can vary — check local GMC dealer inventory.

Safety Record · NHTSA Data

Hummer EV Recalls & Safety

The Hummer EV has had NHTSA recall activity as an early EV platform. Notable recalls include issues with the Ultium battery software, door glass, and the battery management system. Most have been addressed via OTA or dealer service.

Battery Management System OTA Early 2024 · ~14,000 units
Ultium battery management calibration issue; could affect charging behavior. OTA remedy.

  • Check Ultium battery software version (Settings → Vehicle Info)

Ownership Overview

Who Is the GMC Hummer EV For?

Technology enthusiasts and performance buyers who want the most capable and unique EV truck/SUV combination available. Buyers who want something categorically different — not primarily concerned with value or practicality.

✅ STRENGTHS
  • ✓ 1,000hp tri-motor AWD — 0-60 in approximately 3.0 seconds
  • ✓ Crab Walk: all four wheels steer diagonally for tight-space maneuvering
  • ✓ Extract Mode raises ride height 6 inches for off-road obstacle clearance
  • ✓ Available as both pickup truck and SUV (2 body styles)
  • ✓ UltraVision camera system with 18 cameras for off-road visibility
⚠ CONSIDERATIONS
  • ⚠ Extremely heavy (9,000+ lbs) — reduces off-road agility vs lighter competitors
  • ⚠ Edition 1 pricing ($112K+) well above Cybertruck
  • ⚠ Large dimensions challenge urban parking (86.7" wide)
  • ⚠ Below-average efficiency for its class due to extreme weight
  • ⚠ Long wait times and limited production at Factory ZERO

FAQ · Schema Marked Up

GMC Hummer EV — Common Questions

The most searched questions — answered with data.

Crab Walk is a low-speed driving mode that steers all four wheels in the same direction simultaneously — allowing the vehicle to move diagonally. This helps navigate tight trails, parking situations, or off-road obstacles by moving the body laterally. It operates at speeds below 8 mph. It is a genuine utility feature for technical off-road situations and tight parking, not just a gimmick.
Hummer EV advantages: Crab Walk, Extract Mode (6-inch lift), 18-camera UltraVision system, more established GM dealer network. Cybertruck advantages: lower starting price ($99,990 vs $98,845, similar entry), larger bed, more charging network coverage (Tesla Supercharger), and better resale data. Both are extreme EV trucks. Cybertruck is more truck-practical; Hummer EV is more off-road-focused and has more unique features.
The Hummer EV 3X Pickup is EPA-rated at 381 miles — the longest range in the Hummer lineup. The 2X is rated at 329 miles (pickup) and 314 miles (SUV). Real-world range is typically 10–15% below EPA at highway speeds. The vehicle's extreme weight (over 9,000 lbs) means towing significantly reduces range — estimates suggest 50–60% range reduction when towing near maximum capacity.
Yes — the Hummer EV pickup has a front trunk (frunk) with approximately 11 cubic feet of storage. The SUV version has additional interior cargo flexibility. The pickup bed is shorter than traditional trucks due to the mid-mounted battery pack.
The GMC Hummer EV is assembled at GM's Factory ZERO in Detroit, Michigan — a fully electric vehicle-dedicated facility formerly known as Detroit-Hamtramck Assembly. Factory ZERO was GM's first dedicated EV plant, representing a significant investment in American EV manufacturing.
