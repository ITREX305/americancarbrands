Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best tires for Ford F-150?
The best all-season tire for most F-150 owners is a highway all-terrain tire like the BFGoodrich All-Terrain T/A KO2 or Michelin LTX A/T2. For highway-only driving, the Michelin Defender LTX M/S delivers exceptional tread life and comfort.
What size tires does the Ford F-150 use?
F-150 tires vary by trim: XL and XLT typically use 265/70R17 or 275/55R20. Lariat and above often use 275/55R20 or 275/65R18. Always check your door placard for the correct size for your specific trim and year.
How long do F-150 tires last?
Premium all-season tires like Michelin Defender LTX last 60,000–80,000 miles on F-150s used primarily for highway driving. All-terrain tires like the KO2 average 50,000–65,000 miles. Off-road focused tires wear faster in highway use: 30,000–45,000 miles.
Are all-terrain tires worth it for F-150?
For owners who use their F-150 off-road more than 20% of the time, yes. All-terrain tires provide traction in mud, gravel, and light snow that all-season highway tires can't match. Highway noise is the trade-off — expect 3–5 dB more road noise than comparable all-seasons.
What tires do F-150 Raptor owners use?
The F-150 Raptor comes standard on 37-inch BFGoodrich All-Terrain T/A KO2 tires — the same tire ranked best-in-class for off-road capability. Non-Raptor owners fitting 37-inch tires should verify suspension clearance and may need a leveling kit.
Should I get LT or P-rated tires for my F-150?
For towing and hauling applications, LT (Light Truck) tires are recommended — they're built for heavier loads and run higher pressures. For light-duty F-150s that rarely carry heavy loads, P-rated tires offer better ride comfort and fuel economy.
