Ownership Overview · 2026

Who Is the Edge For?

Used car buyers looking for a practical 2-row midsize SUV at a significant discount. The Edge is a known quantity — 18 years of production, wide service network, abundant used inventory. The ST is a particularly good used buy for performance SUV buyers.

✅ Strengths
  • ✓ Wide used inventory — prices have dropped post-discontinuation
  • ✓ Edge ST 335hp is a genuinely fun performance SUV
  • ✓ Good cargo space (73.4 cu ft with seats folded)
  • ✓ Smooth, refined ride quality
  • ✓ Strong dealer service network despite discontinuation
⚠ Weaknesses
  • ⚠ Discontinued — no new models coming, parts supply eventually becomes concern
  • ⚠ Below-average reliability on 2019-2021 models (transmission issues)
  • ⚠ 2.0L base engine can feel strained
  • ⚠ Outdated interior on pre-2021 models
👤 Typical Owner Profile
Practical buyers seeking value in the used market. Buyers who want a midsize SUV between an Escape and Explorer without the Explorer's reliability concerns. ST buyers who want a fun performance SUV at $30-35K used.

Ford Edge — Common Questions

The most searched questions about the Edge — answered with data.

Is the Ford Edge discontinued?+

Yes. Ford discontinued the Edge after the 2024 model year. Production ended at the Oakville Assembly Plant in Ontario in 2024. Ford cited the shift toward Explorer and Escape to cover the midsize SUV segment, and the Bronco for the adventure-oriented buyers the Edge partially served. Used Edge inventory is widely available and prices have dropped post-discontinuation.

Is a used Ford Edge a good buy?+

The Edge is a solid used buy if you stick to the right years: 2015-2018 or 2022-2024. The 2019-2021 generation had documented transmission concerns and build quality issues that drove down used values. The Edge ST with the 2.7L EcoBoost V6 is a particularly good used buy — 335hp performance SUV at $30-35K range.

How much does Edge insurance cost?+

Average full-coverage insurance for a Ford Edge is $152/month. Used Edge insurance is typically $130-145/month depending on the year and condition. The Edge ST costs slightly more at approximately $168/month. Lower MSRP on used examples helps reduce premiums significantly compared to new-vehicle insurance.

What is the Ford Edge ST?+

The Edge ST is the performance variant with a 2.7L EcoBoost V6 producing 335hp and 380 lb-ft of torque. Sport-tuned suspension, flat-bottom steering wheel, and ST-specific interior. It does 0-60 in approximately 5.5 seconds — legitimately quick for a midsize SUV. The ST was one of Ford's best-kept performance secrets: a family SUV that could embarrass sports cars off the line.

What year Edge is most reliable?+

2015-2017 Edge with 2.0L EcoBoost earns Consumer Reports above-average scores. The 2022-2024 Edge improved on the problem-prone 2019-2021 generation. If buying used, target a 2022+ for the most recent improvements, or a 2015-2017 for a proven older example at a lower price. Avoid 2019-2021 Edge models with the 8-speed SelectShift transmission — multiple owners reported shifting issues.

Should I buy a used Edge or new Explorer?+

If budget is tight: a 2022-2024 Edge at $28-34K provides comparable space to an Explorer at a significant saving. If you need 3 rows: Explorer (Edge is 2-row only). If reliability is priority: a 2025 Explorer new or 2015-2017 Edge used. If you want performance: Edge ST used at $30-35K is excellent value versus any current Explorer trim.
