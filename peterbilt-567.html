FAQ

Who makes Peterbilt?
Peterbilt Motors Company — a PACCAR Inc subsidiary (NASDAQ: PCAR). Trucks built in Denton, Texas.
How to buy?
Through authorized Peterbilt dealers. Contact peterbilt.com.
Is Peterbilt reliable?
Yes — consistently top-rated Class 8 trucks in industry reliability surveys.
What is PACCAR?
PACCAR Inc (NASDAQ: PCAR) is parent of both Peterbilt and Kenworth. Headquartered in Bellevue, Washington.
Peterbilt vs Kenworth?
Both are PACCAR brands. Similar components and reliability. Brand preference is often regional or driver-specific.
Is there an electric Peterbilt?
Yes — the 579EV is Peterbilt's Class 8 electric truck for zero-emission regional operations.
