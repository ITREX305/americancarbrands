What Changed

2026 Updates & Changes

⚡ FLASH SALE → DEMAND SURGE → 2027 DELIVERY QUEUE
Tesla briefly offered the base AWD Dual Motor at $59,990 for approximately 10 days in late February 2026. The response was overwhelming — demand exploded so hard that Tesla's configurator for that trim now shows estimated delivery in 2027 for new orders, not late 2026. The price subsequently rose to $69,990 (~$72,235 est. with destination/fees) effective approximately March 1, 2026.

The Cyberbeast tri-motor dropped from $119,990 to ~$99,990, with most configurations landing around $101,985–$102,000 after adjustments (varies by options). A Premium AWD tier at ~$79,990 (~$82,235 with fees) also exists between the two.

⚠ Prices are editorially observed and subject to change without notice. Always verify at tesla.com ↗ before any purchase decision.
🎉 RECENT SOFTWARE WINS — OTA UPDATES
  • Update 2026.2.6.1 (early March 2026): Active Road Noise Cancellation (ANC) went live. Uses microphones + speakers to actively cancel low-frequency road noise. Owners on forums are calling it a game-changer — one of the most significant NVH improvements since launch. The Cybertruck's stainless cabin was notoriously loud at highway speed; this addresses it directly.
  • Oct 2025: Parking light brightness correction (recall remedy via OTA)
  • Ongoing: Autopilot/FSD behavior refinements, range efficiency tuning
⚠ FSD TRANSFER POLICY REVERSAL — MARCH 2026
Tesla reversed its FSD (Full Self-Driving) transfer policy again in March 2026. The new rule requires delivery by March 31, 2026 (not just order by March 31) to qualify for FSD transfer eligibility. Cybertruck AWD buyers who ordered recently and expected to transfer FSD from another Tesla were caught off guard — given the 2027 delivery estimates now showing for new AWD orders, this effectively ends the transfer window for many buyers. This is generating significant community frustration. If FSD transfer is part of your purchase calculus, verify current policy directly at tesla.com/support/fsd-transfer ↗.
RANGE REALITY: EPA vs REAL WORLD
The EPA-rated ~325 miles (AWD) is a combined city/highway test. In the real world, independent tests and owner data consistently show 260–290 miles at highway speed (65–75 mph) unloaded. Cold weather (below 30°F) can push that further to 200–230 miles. The Cyberbeast is similar: ~320 mi EPA, ~255–285 mi real-world highway. These figures are in line with other large-format EVs but can surprise first-time EV truck buyers expecting EPA numbers on the highway.
Source: Aggregated third-party EV range tests and owner reports, March 2026 · EPA figures via fueleconomy.gov · Individual results vary

Ownership Overview

Who Is the Tesla Cybertruck For?

Technology enthusiasts and Tesla loyalists who want something categorically different. Buyers needing high towing capacity with EV running costs. Not ideal for buyers who value conventional repairability or styling.

✅ STRENGTHS
  • ✓ 301 stainless steel body — extreme dent & corrosion resistance
  • ✓ 11,000 lb tow rating (Cyberbeast)
  • ✓ Built-in 120V/240V outlets in bed (up to 11.5kW vehicle-to-load)
  • ✓ Rear-wheel steer improves turning radius significantly
  • ✓ 2,850L total storage: frunk, vault, and under-bed
  • ✓ Supercharger network — largest fast-charge network in North America
⚠ CONSIDERATIONS
  • ⚠ 95.7" wide — does not fit single-car garages or many parking spaces
  • ⚠ Stainless steel body unrepairable by conventional body shops
  • ⚠ 5 NHTSA recalls 2024–2025 (see Recalls section)
  • ⚠ Towing range drops to ~150–175 miles under heavy load
  • ⚠ Polarizing design limits resale audience
  • ⚠ Prices have fluctuated significantly — verify before ordering

Safety Record · NHTSA Data

Cybertruck Recalls & Safety Record

The Cybertruck has experienced a higher-than-average number of recalls for a new model. Listed below are all major NHTSA recall actions. Most have been addressed via OTA software updates or authorized service.

Front Parking Lights Too Bright
OCT 2025 · ~63,619 units
Parking lights exceed SAE/FMVSS illumination standards, potentially impairing other drivers' vision.
✓ Remedy: OTA software update (no dealer visit required)
Off-Road Light Bar Detachment
OCT 2025 · ~6,200 units
Auxiliary off-road light bar can detach while driving, creating a road hazard.
⚠ Remedy: Dealer service — physical inspection and re-torque of mounting hardware
Cant Rail & Exterior Trim Detachment
EARLY 2025 · Nearly all Cybertrucks at time
Body trim panels and cant rail trim can detach at highway speeds, creating road hazard and debris risk.
⚠ Remedy: Dealer service — inspection and re-adhesion/replacement of affected trim
Accelerator Pedal Cover Detachment
2024 · ~3,878 units
Pedal cover could detach and wedge under brake pedal, preventing full braking. High-severity issue.
✓ Remedy: Dealer service (parts replacement) — most units addressed
Windshield Wiper Motor
2024 · ~2,400 units
Wiper motor may fail, impairing visibility in wet conditions.
✓ Remedy: OTA update + dealer motor replacement where needed
ⓘ Recall data sourced from NHTSA.gov as editorially observed. Check your specific VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗ to verify recall completion status on any specific vehicle. Sources: NHTSA.gov · Independent editorial research.

Tesla Cybertruck — Common Questions

The most searched Cybertruck questions — answered with current data.

Tesla chose 301 stainless steel for extreme dent and corrosion resistance. The alloy is harder than conventional automotive steel, eliminating the need for painting. Tradeoff: stainless is extremely difficult to repair — most body shops cannot work on it. All repairs must go through Tesla service centers.
As of March 2026, the base AWD Dual Motor starts at approximately $69,990 (~$72,235 est. with fees). Tesla introduced this new lower base trim in February 2026 at $59,990 initially, adjusting to $69,990 effective approximately March 1, 2026. The Cyberbeast tri-motor dropped to approximately $99,990 (~$102,235 with fees). Always verify current pricing at tesla.com as prices change without notice.
Towing significantly reduces EV range. With a heavy trailer (8,000–10,000 lbs), real-world range drops to approximately 150–175 miles for the Cyberbeast — roughly half the unloaded range. For the AWD trim, expect similar proportional reduction. The optional range extender adds additional capacity. This is comparable to other electric trucks when towing heavy loads.
The Cybertruck has experienced five NHTSA recalls from 2024 to late 2025, covering the accelerator pedal cover (high severity), exterior trim detachment, windshield wiper motor, parking light brightness, and off-road light bar detachment. Early 2024 VINs had above-average issue rates. 2025+ production has addressed several items. Buyers considering any used Cybertruck should verify recall completion status at nhtsa.gov/recalls before purchase.
The Cybertruck is 95.7 inches wide and 218.8 inches long — wider than most full-size trucks. A standard 2-car garage door (16 ft wide) accommodates the Cybertruck, but single-car garages (8–9 ft) typically do not. Measure your garage before ordering.
The Rivian R1T starts at a similar price (~$69,000) and offers superior EPA range (~410 miles for Max Pack) — and remains the off-road capability leader with air suspension standard on most trims, better approach/departure angles, and a more proven off-road track record. The R1T fits in standard garages and has a conventional truck body most shops can work on. The Cybertruck has the Tesla Supercharger network advantage and more raw power in Cyberbeast form (845 hp). For off-road adventurers or buyers prioritizing max range, R1T has a meaningful edge. For Tesla ecosystem users, Supercharger access, or those who want the unique stainless design, Cybertruck wins.
In late February 2026, Tesla briefly dropped the base AWD to $59,990 for roughly 10 days. The response was extraordinary — orders flooded in at a volume Tesla apparently did not anticipate. When the price rose to $69,990 on approximately March 1, 2026, Tesla's configurator began showing 2027 delivery estimates for new AWD orders. This is not a production problem per se, but rather demand outrunning near-term build slots. If you want a 2026 delivery, you either need to find an existing inventory unit, or order a trim with faster availability (Cyberbeast availability may differ). Verify current delivery estimates at tesla.com/cybertruck.
Tesla reversed its FSD (Full Self-Driving) transfer policy in March 2026. Previously, buyers who ordered by March 31 could transfer FSD. The policy now requires vehicle delivery by March 31, 2026. For Cybertruck AWD buyers whose orders now show 2027 delivery estimates, this policy shift effectively eliminates the FSD transfer window entirely. This has generated significant frustration in the owner community. Before purchasing, verify current FSD transfer eligibility directly at tesla.com/support/fsd-transfer ↗. Policy details are editorial observations — confirm with Tesla directly.
