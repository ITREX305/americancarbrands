Editorial Verdict

Which Should You Choose?

These two compete directly but excel in different areas. Choose the Grand Cherokee if you need serious off-road capability (Trailhawk), want the 4xe PHEV with 26-mile electric range, or prioritize towing (7,200 lbs vs 5,600 lbs). Choose the Explorer if you need a genuine 3-row 7-passenger layout, want the better highway fuel economy, or prefer a lower entry price. The Explorer is the better family hauler; the Grand Cherokee is the better off-roader and tow vehicle.

The Grand Cherokee tows more: 7,200 lbs (V6) vs 5,600 lbs (Explorer EcoBoost). For families who tow boats, campers, or trailers, the Grand Cherokee's advantage is meaningful.
