Electric Powertrain

The EDV 700 uses Rivian's commercial electric platform optimized for high-cycle urban delivery routes. Regenerative braking maximizes efficiency on stop-and-go routes.

Urban Delivery Efficiency

Electric powertrains excel in urban last-mile delivery — stop-and-go routes maximize regenerative braking benefits while eliminating fuel costs.

05

Fleet Operations

The EDV 700 is purpose-built for delivery fleet operations: high roof for driver ergonomics, large cargo capacity, and range designed for urban route cycles.

Fleet Design

Every design decision in the EDV 700 prioritizes fleet operator efficiency — driver ergonomics, cargo throughput, and depot charging integration.

06

Amazon Partnership

Amazon placed a landmark order of 100,000 EDV vans from Rivian — one of the largest electric vehicle orders in history. This order validated Rivian's commercial vehicle platform and funded the company's growth.

100,000-Van Order

FAQ

Can I buy a Rivian EDV 700?
No — the EDV 700 is only available to commercial fleet operators. It is not sold to individual consumers.
What is the EDV 700?
A purpose-built electric delivery van by Rivian, launched through a partnership with Amazon. Not a consumer vehicle.
How many EDVs does Amazon have?
Amazon ordered 100,000 EDVs from Rivian — one of the largest EV orders in history.
How far can it go?
Approximately 170 miles per charge — designed for urban last-mile delivery routes.
Where is it built?
Normal, Illinois at Rivian's manufacturing facility.
How does EDV compare to consumer Rivian?
The R1T and R1S are consumer vehicles available for individual purchase. The EDV 700 is a commercial-only fleet product.
