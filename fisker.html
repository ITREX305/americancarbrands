Brand Overview

Fisker Inc. filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in June 2024. Production of the Fisker Ocean SUV has permanently ended. Approximately 10,000 Ocean SUVs were produced before the bankruptcy. Unsold inventory was auctioned at significant discounts during the bankruptcy proceedings.

Status: Bankrupt (Chapter 11, June 2024). Stock delisted from NYSE — now OTC. HQ: Manhattan Beach, California. Henrik Fisker founded the company in 2016 as a second attempt at an EV startup (the first, Fisker Automotive, went bankrupt in 2013).

If you own a Fisker Ocean: OTA software updates have ceased. Warranty coverage is uncertain — submit claims through the bankruptcy court process. Service centers associated with Fisker may be closed. Find independent service shops familiar with EV platforms (the Ocean uses a Magna International platform). Check nhtsa.gov/recalls for any open recalls — some may require physical service that is now difficult to obtain.

We recommend against purchasing a Fisker Ocean at any price unless you have thoroughly investigated: (1) all open NHTSA recalls and whether they can be resolved without Fisker infrastructure; (2) independent service shop availability in your area; (3) parts availability from Magna International; (4) the OTA update situation — the vehicle may have outdated/unsupported software permanently. FAQ

What happened to Fisker Inc.?
Fisker Inc. filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy in June 2024. The company was unable to secure additional financing to continue operations after slower-than-projected sales and a failed acquisition attempt by a major automaker. Production of the Fisker Ocean permanently ended. This was Henrik Fisker's second EV company to go bankrupt — the first, Fisker Automotive, failed in 2013.
Is it safe to buy a used Fisker Ocean?
The purchase of a used Fisker Ocean carries significant risks that most used EVs do not: (1) no manufacturer OTA updates; (2) limited service center network; (3) uncertain parts availability long-term; (4) multiple open NHTSA recalls that may be difficult to resolve. If you proceed, verify all open recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls and research independent service availability in your area before purchasing.
Is the Fisker Ocean warranty still valid?
As of March 2026, Fisker Inc. is bankrupt and not honoring standard warranty claims. If you believe you have a valid warranty claim, you should contact the bankruptcy trustee managing the Fisker estate. Warranty coverage from a bankrupt manufacturer is generally treated as an unsecured creditor claim in bankruptcy proceedings.
Are there recalls on the Fisker Ocean?
Yes — multiple. Fisker Ocean had numerous NHTSA investigations and safety recalls filed before and during the bankruptcy process. With Fisker bankrupt and service centers largely closed, resolving these recalls may be difficult. Check nhtsa.gov/recalls with your VIN for all open recalls on your specific vehicle.
Who was Henrik Fisker?
Henrik Fisker is a Danish-American automotive designer and entrepreneur, previously lead designer at BMW and Aston Martin. He founded Fisker Automotive in 2007 (bankrupt 2013), then Fisker Inc. in 2016 (bankrupt 2024). He is widely regarded as a gifted designer — the Fisker Ocean won multiple design awards. His ventures have struggled with execution, scaling, and financial management.
