Overview

The 2025 Rivian R1T is America's original electric pickup — launched in 2021, it predates the Lightning and Cybertruck. Rivian builds the R1T in Normal, Illinois, and it remains the most adventure-focused electric truck available.

Large Pack EPA range ~352 miles; at 70 mph expect ~274 miles. Cold weather reduces range ~15–20%. Towing at max capacity reduces range 45–55% — budget 175–200 miles per charge when towing near max.

Adventure Planning

Rivian's Trip Planner app integrates charging stops into routes automatically. For off-road adventures, the R1T can drive to remote areas and recover most range overnight on a 240V campsite hookup.

The Max Pack delivers 410 miles EPA — the longest range of any electric pickup. DC fast charging at 200 kW adds approximately 140 miles in 20 minutes. NACS connector is standard across all configurations.

The Large Pack (352 miles) is the most popular choice — adequate range for most adventure trips without the premium of the Max Pack.

05

Towing & Off-Road

11,000 lb Towing

Towing 11,000 lb reduces range 50–55%. For a 200-mile towing trip, plan at least one DC fast charge stop. Rivian recommends charging to 100% (vs daily 80%) for towing days.

Max tow of 11,000 lb exceeds the Ford F-150 Lightning (9,100 lb) and Cybertruck AWD (11,000 lb, tied). Air suspension lowers for easy trailer hookup. Rivian's Trailer Backup Assist manages trailer angle intuitively.

Air suspension, standard locking differentials, and a wade mode (water fording up to 34 inches) make the R1T one of the most capable off-road electric trucks available.

06

Unique Features

Gear Tunnel Advantage

No other truck — gas or electric — has a lockable, weatherproof pass-through storage tunnel between the cab and bed. This is genuinely useful for overlanding, camping, and keeping gear separated from tools.

The Gear Tunnel is a lockable pass-through storage compartment behind the cab — unique to the R1T globally. It fits two sets of skis, a tool set, a camp kitchen, or a compressed air compressor (standard on Adventure+ package).

Camp Kitchen slides out of the Gear Tunnel and includes a two-burner stove, fridge, and sink — an optional accessory unique to Rivian's ecosystem.

FAQ

What is the 2025 R1T starting price?
Standard Pack Dual starts at $67,900. Large Pack at ~$73,900. Max Pack Quad at ~$86,900. Prices exclude destination. The R1T may qualify for $7,500 federal EV tax credit — verify with Rivian.
How far does the Rivian R1T go?
Max Pack: 410 miles EPA. Large Pack: ~352 miles EPA. Standard Pack: ~270 miles estimated. At 70 mph, expect approximately 78% of EPA range.
How much can the R1T tow?
11,000 lb on Large and Max Pack configurations. Standard Pack is rated at 7,700 lb. Towing near max capacity reduces range 45–55%.
What is the Gear Tunnel?
A lockable, weatherproof storage compartment that passes through the vehicle between the cab and bed. Unique to the R1T globally — fits skis, tools, a camp kitchen, or gear that needs to be separated from the open bed.
Is the Rivian R1T reliable?
Better than early production, but still below established automaker standards. 2023+ production is significantly more reliable than 2021–2022 launch vehicles. Extended warranty is recommended.
What EV tax credits apply?

Electric vehicles assembled in North America may qualify for up to $7,500 in federal EV tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Income limits and MSRP caps apply. Verify eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxevb.shtml before purchasing.
