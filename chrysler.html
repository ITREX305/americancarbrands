Independent editorial research on Chrysler — not affiliated with Stellantis. All figures are editorial estimates compiled from publicly available sources.

Chrysler is an American automaker founded in 1925, now operating as a division of Stellantis. Once a full-line brand competing with Ford and GM, Chrysler today focuses primarily on the Pacifica minivan. The brand’s history includes iconic vehicles like the 300 and the original minivan revolution of 1984.

Brand Heritage

The Chrysler Story

Editorial brand history. Not affiliated with or reviewed by Stellantis.

Walter P. Chrysler founded the Chrysler Corporation in 1925 by reorganizing the Maxwell Motor Company. Chrysler became the #2 US automaker by 1937, peaking in market share during the 1950s. The 1984 Dodge Caravan/Plymouth Voyager invented the minivan category. After near-bankruptcy in 2009, Chrysler merged with Fiat, forming what is now Stellantis.

Corporate Structure

Who Owns Chrysler?

Editorial corporate intelligence. Stock tickers referenced for identification only — not investment advice.

Chrysler is owned by Stellantis (NYSE: STLA), a multinational automotive group formed in 2021 from the merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA) and PSA Group. Stellantis also owns Dodge, Jeep, Ram, Alfa Romeo, Maserati, Peugeot, Citroën, and Fiat, among other brands.

🚐 Pacifica — The Last Chrysler Standing Model Intel
+

The Chrysler Pacifica is essentially Chrysler's entire US product lineup as of 2026. The 300 sedan was discontinued in 2023. The Pacifica Hybrid (PHEV) is widely praised as one of the most practical family vehicles available — 32 miles of EV range, 80+ MPGe, seating for 7–8, and federal tax credit eligibility. Built in Windsor, Ontario, Canada.

❓ Is Chrysler Going Out of Business? Brand Future
+

Chrysler is not going out of business, but its future as a standalone brand is uncertain. Stellantis CEO Carlos Tavares has discussed brand rationalization. The Chrysler Halcyon EV concept (shown 2024) signals Stellantis's intent to keep the nameplate alive. Without a confirmed new product lineup beyond the Pacifica, the brand's long-term roadmap remains unclear as of 2026.

🏛️ Chrysler History — Bankruptcy, Mergers & Stellantis Corporate
+

Walter P. Chrysler founded the company in 1925. Chrysler went bankrupt in 2009, was acquired by Fiat, became Fiat Chrysler Automobiles (FCA), then merged with PSA Group to form Stellantis in 2021. Throughout this history, Chrysler transitioned from a full-line automaker to a brand with a single product. The Chrysler 300 was discontinued in 2023.

Common Questions About Chrysler

Frequently asked questions about Chrysler — answered with editorial research. Not affiliated with Stellantis.

Is Chrysler still making cars? +

As of 2026, Chrysler primarily produces the Pacifica minivan. The brand's car lineup has shrunk significantly over the years. Chrysler's future product roadmap under Stellantis has not confirmed major new car launches beyond minivan variants.

Who owns Chrysler? +

Chrysler is owned by Stellantis N.V. (NYSE: STLA), a multinational automaker formed from the 2021 merger of Fiat Chrysler Automobiles and the French PSA Group.

Is Chrysler reliable? +

Chrysler reliability ratings are generally below average. The Pacifica has had mixed reliability reviews, with the plug-in hybrid version receiving more complaints than the standard version.

Where are Chrysler vehicles made? +

The Chrysler Pacifica is assembled in Windsor, Ontario, Canada at the Windsor Assembly Plant.

What happened to the Chrysler 300? +

The Chrysler 300 was discontinued after the 2023 model year. Stellantis has not announced a direct replacement as of 2026.

Chrysler Recalls & Safety Notices

⚠ Always verify recalls on your specific VIN at NHTSA.gov/recalls — this section is editorial context only.
🔍 Chrysler Recall Overview & Context +

Chrysler (Stellantis) has had notable recalls on Pacifica Hybrid involving potential fire risk from battery system — check VIN before purchasing used. Some units required dealer inspection.

ⓘ Editorial summary only. Not a substitute for official NHTSA VIN lookup. Check your VIN at NHTSA ↗

📋 How to Check Chrysler Recalls by VIN +
  1. Go to nhtsa.gov/recalls
  2. Enter your 17-digit VIN (found on dashboard, door jamb, or title)
  3. Review all open recalls — note which have been remediated
  4. Contact an authorized Chrysler dealer to complete any open recalls at no cost
ⓘ Recall repairs are always free at authorized dealers, regardless of vehicle age, mileage, or ownership status.
📱 OTA vs Dealer Recall Repair — What’s the Difference? +
📱 OTA Update
Software-only fix pushed wirelessly. No dealer visit required. Common on Tesla, GM (Super Cruise), Ford (Sync), and newer models.
🔧 Dealer Repair
Physical parts replacement or mechanical repair required. Must schedule dealer appointment. Always free under recall.

ⓘ NHTSA determines recall remedy type. OTA-resolved recalls still appear in NHTSA history — check that your vehicle's remedy has been applied.
