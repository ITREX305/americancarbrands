Overview

The 2026 Dodge Durango starts at $41,590 and competes as a 3-row suv. The Durango SRT Hellcat is the most powerful three-row SUV ever put into production, using the same supercharged 6.2L Hemi V8 found in Dodge's most extreme muscle cars to deliver 710 hp in a genuinely practical family hauler.

SRT Hellcat trim, supercharged V8 headlines the spec sheet, and across the lineup buyers can expect 295-710 hp depending on trim and configuration. Cross-shoppers researching this segment should compare total cost of ownership, not just sticker price — insurance, projected depreciation, and fuel or charging costs all vary meaningfully between competitors at this price point.

Who It's For

The Durango suits buyers who specifically want srt hellcat trim, supercharged v8 without stepping up to a more expensive trim or competing nameplate. Verify current incentives and regional pricing at your local Dodge dealer, as manufacturer rebates shift monthly.
