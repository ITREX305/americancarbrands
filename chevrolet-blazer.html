Overview

The 2025 Chevrolet Blazer sits between the Equinox and Traverse in the Chevrolet SUV lineup. The gas Blazer targets sporty mid-size crossover buyers; the Blazer EV targets those wanting an American EV alternative to Tesla.

Dual Strategy

Gas Blazer and Blazer EV target different buyers under one nameplate. GM uses this approach to maximize dealer throughput and brand recognition while transitioning customers from ICE to EV.

RS V6 Value

At $44,495, the RS V6 with AWD and 308 hp is a legitimate bargain in the mid-size crossover segment. The V6 delivers more punch than most competitors' base engines.

Gas Blazer offers two engines: the 2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder (228 hp) on LT, or the 3.6L V6 (308 hp) on RS. The V6 RS feels much quicker and adds AWD as standard.

05

Blazer EV

Blazer EV SS Performance

557 hp and 3.4-second 0–60 makes the Blazer EV SS one of the quickest mid-size crossovers available — faster than the Model Y Performance in this configuration.

The Blazer EV LT starts at $44,995 with 340 miles of EPA range — exceptional for the price. The SS dual-motor produces 557 hp and 0–60 in 3.4 seconds — genuinely fast for a mid-size crossover.

The EV charges at up to 190 kW DC fast (SS), adding ~70 miles in 10 minutes.

Tech & Styling

Design Identity

The Blazer's bold styling — wide stance, horizontal light bar, and available red interior — gives it clear visual identity in a segment of conservative-looking crossovers.

All Blazer models feature a 17.7-inch diagonal LVDS touchscreen (on RS/EV trims) with Google built-in. Sport styling with horizontal lighting signatures and available red interior accents give the Blazer a distinctive look.

FAQ

What is the 2025 Blazer starting price?
Gas LT starts at $38,995. RS V6 ~$44,495. Blazer EV LT starts at $44,995. EV SS ~$56,995. Prices exclude destination (~$1,395).
What is the difference between Blazer and Blazer EV?
The gas Blazer uses turbocharged 4-cylinder or V6 engines. The Blazer EV is a dedicated electric vehicle with different platform architecture, up to 340-mile range, and available 557-hp dual-motor.
How far does the Blazer EV go?
EV LT (FWD) is EPA-rated at 340 miles. EV SS (AWD) is rated at 303 miles. Both use NACS connectors.
Is the Blazer a good family SUV?
For 2 adults and children, yes — it seats 5 and has a practical cargo area. For 6+ people, step up to the 3-row Traverse.
Blazer vs Equinox — what's the difference?
Blazer is slightly larger, sportier styled, and starts higher. Equinox is cheaper, more efficient, and available in a widely praised EV at $34,995. For pure value, Equinox EV wins; for performance, Blazer RS V6 or Blazer EV SS win.
