Tesla
Austin, TX · Founded 2003
The company that proved EVs could be fast, beautiful, and aspirational. From a near-bankrupt startup in 2008 to the world's most valuable automaker. Model S, 3, X, Y, Cybertruck, Semi. Still holds ~45% of US EV market share.
CEOElon Musk
Revenue~$97B (2024)
US PlantsFremont CA, Austin TX
Production · ~1.8M vehicles/yr TSLA

Rivian
Irvine, CA · Founded 2009
Michigan-born EV adventure brand with genuine off-road credibility. The R1T pickup and R1S SUV are critically acclaimed. Amazon delivery van contract (100,000 vans) provides commercial stability. Normal, IL factory currently producing.
Key BackerAmazon (20% stake)
2024 Deliveries~51,000 vehicles
PlantNormal, Illinois
Production · Growing RIVN

In Production
Lucid Motors
Newark, CA · Founded 2007
Engineering-first ultra-luxury EV brand. The Lucid Air holds the longest EPA-rated range of any production EV: 516 miles. Backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) with ~60% ownership. The Gravity SUV launched in 2024.
Key BackerSaudi PIF (~60%)
Record Range516 miles (Air Pure)
PlantCasa Grande, Arizona
Production · Low Volume LCID

Pre-Production
Scout Motors
Columbia, SC · Founded 2022
VW Group-backed revival of the legendary International Harvester Scout nameplate. Electric off-road SUV and pickup truck being built in South Carolina. Enormous community anticipation. Reservations opened in 2024 with strong response.
BackerVolkswagen Group
PlantColumbia, SC (under construction)
Launch Target2027

Canoo
Oklahoma City, OK · Founded 2017
Electric vehicle startup with an innovative skateboard platform and distinctive van-style Lifestyle Vehicle. Early customers included NASA and the US Army. Facing severe financial headwinds and operational challenges. Future uncertain.
Notable ContractsNASA, US Army
StatusCash-constrained, restructuring

Aptera Motors
San Diego, CA · Founded 2006 (refounded 2019)
The solar EV. Three-wheeled, radically aerodynamic vehicle that charges itself from solar panels. Claims 1,000+ mile range. A genuinely different vision of transportation — if it makes it to production. Strong reservation numbers, fundraising ongoing.
Design3-wheel, solar-charged
Claimed RangeUp to 1,000 miles
Pre-Production · Fundraising Private

In Production
Workhorse Group
Cincinnati, OH · Founded 2007
Ohio-based commercial EV manufacturer building electric delivery vans and step vans. Competed — unsuccessfully — for the massive USPS mail truck contract. Continuing with commercial vehicle lineup for last-mile delivery fleets.
FocusCommercial delivery EVs
Production · Commercial WKHS

Fisker Inc.
Los Angeles, CA · Founded 2016
Henrik Fisker's second EV brand. The Fisker Ocean was critically praised at launch — stylish, reasonably priced, promising. But software, service, and financial issues compounded. Filed Chapter 11 in June 2024. Assets acquired by American Lease. A cautionary EV tale.
Filed BankruptcyJune 2024
Vehicles Made~10,000 Ocean SUVs

Lordstown, OH · Founded 2018
Took over GM's iconic Lordstown, Ohio plant with ambitions for the Endurance electric pickup. Pre-orders from fleets looked promising. Production and financial problems mounted. Filed for bankruptcy in 2023 after very limited deliveries of the Endurance.
