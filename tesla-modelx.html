Strengths & Considerations

✅ STRENGTHS
  • ✓ Falcon-wing rear doors — fully unique in the market, excellent for child seats
  • ✓ 7-seat configuration with usable third row for adults
  • ✓ 1,020 hp Plaid — fastest SUV in existence at this spec
  • ✓ HEPA air filtration system (Bioweapon Defense Mode)
  • ✓ Supercharger network — fastest and most reliable EV charging
  • ✓ 17" front touchscreen + rear touchscreens in Plaid
⚠ CONSIDERATIONS
  • ⚠ Falcon-wing doors require vertical clearance — tricky in low-ceiling parking garages
  • ⚠ Below-average reliability due to door mechanism complexity
  • ⚠ $79,990 starting price — most expensive non-Plaid SUV in Tesla lineup
  • ⚠ Does not qualify for federal EV tax credit (may exceed $80K SUV cap)
  • ⚠ Range drops significantly when towing or with full occupancy
  • ⚠ Repair costs high — aluminum structure, proprietary falcon door mechanisms

🔍 USED BUYING CHECKLIST
  • Run VIN at nhtsa.gov/recalls — verify all recalls resolved
  • Request Tesla service history via app or ask for screenshots
  • Check battery health — ask for remaining range at 100% charge
  • Confirm OTA software version is current (Settings → Software)
  • Inspect paint and trim — Tesla QC varies by production year
🔋 BATTERY DEGRADATION
  • Typical 2020–2022 Model X: 5–12% range loss by 50,000–80,000 miles
  • Avoid routine 100% SOC overnight charging to preserve long-term health
  • Tesla battery warranty: 8-year/100,000–150,000 mi (varies by trim) covering >70% retention

Tesla Model X — Common Questions

As of March 2026, the Model X Long Range AWD starts at approximately $79,990 (~$81,380 est. with destination). The Plaid tri-motor is ~$99,990 (~$101,380 with fees). Verify at tesla.com/modelx ↗. Neither trim currently qualifies for the federal EV tax credit — verify current IRA eligibility at irs.gov.
The Model X rear doors open vertically (upward), with two pivot points that allow them to open in as little as 11 inches of clearance — making them more practical than traditional gull-wing doors. Sensors detect nearby obstacles and adjust the opening angle. They require adequate vertical clearance, however — low-ceiling garages below ~6 feet may prevent full opening.
EPA ratings: Long Range AWD ~348 miles, Plaid ~333 miles (5-seat config). Real-world highway range at 70–75 mph is typically 290–310 miles for the LR. With 7 passengers and cargo, expect ~20–30% reduction. Cold weather also reduces range ~20–30%.
Standard configuration is 5 seats. Optional upgrades include a 6-seat layout (captain's chairs in second row) or 7-seat configuration (bench second row). The third row seats are usable by adults under ~5'9" — taller than most EV SUVs' third rows.
The Model X has historically below-average reliability compared to Model 3/Y — primarily due to falcon-wing door mechanism complexity. The 2021+ refresh improved build quality, but the door mechanisms remain the primary reported maintenance item. Motor and battery reliability are strong. Check NHTSA recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls ↗.
