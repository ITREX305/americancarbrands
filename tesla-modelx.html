Overview

The Tesla Model X is Tesla's full-size luxury electric SUV — a 7-passenger vehicle defined by its Falcon-wing rear doors, which open vertically and require minimal side clearance. It is the only production SUV with these doors at any volume. Built at Gigafactory Fremont, California. The Model X competes with the Cadillac Escalade, Lincoln Navigator, and BMW X7 while offering far greater EV range and the unique door mechanism.

FAQ

How do the Tesla Model X Falcon-wing doors work?
The Falcon-wing doors are rear passenger doors that open vertically upward, using articulated hinges that allow them to open with as little as 12 inches of clearance on each side. They use ultrasonic sensors to detect obstacles and adjust the opening angle automatically. They are powered and controlled via the touchscreen, key fob, or app.
What is the Tesla Model X's towing capacity?
Both the Long Range and Plaid Model X are rated to tow up to 5,000 lbs — the highest of any luxury EV SUV. This covers most travel trailers and boat trailers. Expect 40–50% range reduction at maximum tow load; plan charging stops every 100–150 miles.
Is the Falcon-wing door reliable?
It is the most documented reliability concern of the Model X. Sensor calibration issues, actuator failures, and door misalignment have been reported across multiple model years. Tesla has improved the system via OTA updates over time. If purchasing used, budget for potential door service.
How does the Model X compare to the Cadillac Escalade IQ?
The Escalade IQ EV (expected 2026, ~$130K+) will offer an estimated 450+ miles range vs. Model X's 348 miles. The Escalade IQ has a more traditional luxury brand positioning and standard door system. The Model X has Falcon-wing doors, proven production history, and better charging infrastructure access.
Does the Model X have three rows?
Yes — the Model X seats up to 7 passengers in a 3-row configuration (2+3+2) or 6 in a 2+2+2 captain's chair setup. The third row is more usable for adults than the Model Y's 3rd row. Cargo behind the third row is 11.1 cu ft, expanding to 87.6 cu ft with all seats folded.
What EV tax credits apply?

Electric vehicles assembled in North America may qualify for up to $7,500 in federal EV tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Income limits and MSRP caps apply. Verify eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxevb.shtml before purchasing.
