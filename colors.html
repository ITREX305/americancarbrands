How to change the color: Open index.html, ford.html, or tesla.html. Find :root at the top of the CSS. Change three lines: --accent, --accent-dark, and --accent-glow. Every border, button, link, hover state, and highlight will update automatically across the entire page. The gold ticker color and green status indicators are intentionally fixed and do not change.

Recommendation: Cobalt Blue (#1B4FD8) or Burnt Orange (#C95A00) are the strongest alternatives to red. Cobalt reads as authoritative and modern. Orange is the most distinctive — no major auto info site uses it.
