EPA-rated range is tested at moderate speed/temp. Real-world at 70 mph is approximately 343 miles. Cold weather (32°F) reduces range ~22%. Towing near max capacity reduces range 43–53%. Always plan charging stops when towing long distances.

440 Miles in an SUV

Model X Long Range: 348 miles. Rivian R1S Max Pack: 410 miles. Cadillac Escalade IQ: ~450 miles estimated. Gravity: 440 EPA confirmed. Lucid holds the largest EPA range advantage of any EV brand vs its nearest competitor.

The Gravity Grand Touring achieves 440 miles EPA — the first electric SUV of any kind to exceed 400 miles in EPA testing, and significantly ahead of any competitor. Lucid's proprietary motor efficiency extends into the SUV platform with minimal range compromise vs the Air sedan.

05

3-Row Interior

Third Row for Adults

The Gravity's extended wheelbase enables genuine adult third-row legroom — uncommon in luxury electric SUVs where battery packaging typically compromises rear-row space.

Three-row seating for 7 passengers is standard. The Gravity's interior uses aircraft-inspired design with panoramic glass, sustainable materials, and a 34-inch curved display that matches the Air's cabin technology.

Second-row seats fold electrically; third-row access is via power-folding second seats — practical for regular third-row use.

06

Gravity vs Rivals

Range vs Tow Trade-off

Gravity leads on range (440 mi) but trails Rivian R1S on towing (6,000 vs 11,000 lb). For buyers where towing is primary, R1S wins. For buyers where range is primary — road trips, range anxiety — Gravity is the clear choice.

The Gravity at $94,900 base competes with the Rivian R1S ($73,900), Cadillac Escalade IQ (~$130K), and Tesla Model X ($84,990). Gravity wins on range. R1S wins on towing (11,000 lb vs 6,000 lb). Escalade IQ wins on luxury brand prestige.

FAQ

What is the 2025 Lucid Gravity starting price?
Base Gravity starts at $94,900. Grand Touring at $119,900. Prices exclude destination.
How far does the Lucid Gravity go?
Grand Touring: 440 miles EPA — the longest EPA range of any electric SUV ever tested. Base Gravity: approximately 410 miles (estimated; EPA testing pending as of publication).
How does Gravity compare to Rivian R1S?
R1S ($73,900) wins on towing (11,000 vs 6,000 lb) and price. Gravity wins on range (440 vs 410 mi R1S Max Pack) and interior luxury quality. Choose by towing vs range priority.
Does Lucid Gravity have 3 rows?
Yes — 3-row seating for 7 is standard on all Gravity configurations.
Is the Lucid Gravity reliable?
It's brand new (late 2024 launch) with very limited data. Based on the Air platform, early owner reports are cautiously positive. Essential to purchase with extended warranty given Lucid's startup status.
What EV tax credits apply?

Electric vehicles assembled in North America may qualify for up to $7,500 in federal EV tax credits under the Inflation Reduction Act. Income limits and MSRP caps apply. Verify eligibility at fueleconomy.gov/feg/taxevb.shtml before purchasing.
