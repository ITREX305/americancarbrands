Overview

It outsells every other vehicle in the world, including the Ford F-150. Built at Fremont, CA and Gigafactory Texas (Austin). The most practical Tesla for families and the best all-around EV at its price point.

FAQ

Is the Tesla Model Y the best-selling car in the world?
Yes — the Tesla Model Y was the world's best-selling vehicle outright in 2023, surpassing even the Toyota Corolla and Toyota RAV4. It was the first electric vehicle to achieve this distinction globally.
Can the Model Y tow a trailer?
The Model Y Long Range AWD and Performance are rated to tow up to 3,500 lbs with the optional tow hitch. This covers jet skis, small boats, and light trailers. Expect a 35–45% range reduction when towing at max load.
Does the Tesla Model Y have a third row?
Yes — an optional 3rd row (5+2 seating) is available on LR AWD and Performance. The third row seats two people but is small — better suited for children than adults. It folds flat when not in use.
How does the Model Y compare to the Ford Mustang Mach-E?
The Model Y has longer range (330 mi vs. ~312 mi for the Mach-E Extended Range), better charging infrastructure (Supercharger vs. NACS but less coverage for Ford), and more cargo space. The Mach-E has CarPlay/Android Auto, more conventional interior, and Ford dealer service network.
What is the difference between Model Y and Model 3?
The Model Y is a compact SUV — taller, longer, more cargo space (68 vs. 23 cu ft), higher seating position, and available third row. The Model 3 is a lower, sportier sedan with better range per dollar. The Model 3 starts $4,750 less. Most families prefer the Y for practicality; the 3 for driving feel.
