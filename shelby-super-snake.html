Overview

The Shelby Super Snake carries the most storied name in American performance. Carroll Shelby's legacy lives on at Shelby American's Las Vegas facility, where each Super Snake is hand-assembled with the same obsessive attention to detail that defined the original GT350 and GT500 of the 1960s.

The 2026 Super Snake starts with a Ford Mustang GT and transforms it into something altogether different. The 825 hp supercharged package breathes through a Whipple 3.0L TVS supercharger bolted to the 5.0L Coyote V8 — producing power figures that overlap with European supercars costing twice as much. Shelby backs it with a full 3-year/36,000-mile warranty, which no other builder at this performance level can claim.

Each Super Snake receives a Shelby serialized dash plaque, is registered in the official Shelby World Registry, and comes with a Shelby-signed window sticker. Resale values have historically tracked above MSRP for low-mileage examples — the Super Snake is as much an investment as a driver's car.

The wide-body conversion adds 3.5 inches of track width per side, accommodating massive 305/30/20 rear rubber. Shelby's handling package includes adjustable coilovers, upgraded Brembo brakes, and a Shelby-tuned stability control calibration. This isn't just power — it's a complete performance transformation. Competition at this price includes the Dodge Charger Daytona SRT, BMW M4 CS, and Mercedes-AMG C63, none of which match the Super Snake's horsepower figure or collector pedigree.

Costs & Comparison

Ownership Costs

Super Snake ownership involves significant insurance premiums — $3,500–$6,000 annually. Whipple supercharger oil changes are every 30,000 miles; the Coyote V8 requires oil every 5,000 miles with full synthetic at $180–$220. Track use requires additional brake pads and tire budgets. Shelby's 3-year warranty provides meaningful protection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many Shelby Super Snakes are built each year?
Shelby American limits Super Snake production to maintain exclusivity and value. Exact production numbers vary annually, but typically several hundred units are produced per year across all configurations. Each receives a unique serial number registered in the Shelby World Registry.
Is the Shelby Super Snake street legal?
Yes — the Super Snake is fully street legal in all 50 states. It passes all emissions requirements and comes with a standard Monroney sticker. The 825 hp tune is emissions-compliant through Shelby's CARB-exempt calibration.
Does the Shelby Super Snake hold its value?
Super Snakes have historically held value well, with low-mileage examples often trading at or above original MSRP. Serialized, documented examples with original Shelby paperwork command the strongest prices at auction.
What warranty does the Super Snake come with?
The Super Snake includes Shelby American's 3-year/36,000-mile limited warranty covering Shelby-installed components, plus the underlying Ford Mustang factory warranty for base vehicle components.
How does the Super Snake compare to a stock Shelby GT500?
The Super Snake significantly surpasses the GT500 in power (825 hp vs 760 hp) and is a more exclusive vehicle. The GT500 is a Ford production car; the Super Snake is a hand-built Shelby American product with serialized provenance.
Can you track a Shelby Super Snake?
Yes, but track use requires preparation. Shelby recommends upgraded brake fluid, fresh pads, and tire pressure adjustments for track sessions. The Speed Package is optimized for track use with additional cooling and suspension upgrades.
Is the 2026 Shelby Super Snake a good investment?
The Shelby Super Snake has a strong track record as a collector vehicle. Numbered, documented examples in original condition have appreciated. However, past performance does not guarantee future results. Consult a collector car specialist before purchasing as an investment.
What does the Shelby Super Snake weigh?
The Shelby Super Snake weighs approximately 4,000 lbs depending on configuration — comparable to a stock Mustang GT with the wide-body and supercharger components.
