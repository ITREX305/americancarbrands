516-Mile Range Engineering

EPA-rated range is tested at moderate speed/temp. Real-world at 70 mph is approximately 319 miles. Cold weather (32°F) reduces range ~18%. Towing near max capacity reduces range 40–50%. Always plan charging stops when towing long distances.

Efficiency Secret

Lucid's motors achieve 93% efficiency vs ~88% for competitors. This ~5% advantage doesn't sound significant, but at vehicle scale it translates to 50+ more miles from the same battery size. It's the reason Lucid holds the range record.

The Grand Touring's 516-mile EPA range is achieved through Lucid's proprietary motor technology — the most efficient electric motors in any production vehicle. A 118-kWh battery delivers range that no other EV — Tesla, Rivian, or European — has approached in EPA testing.

05

900V Architecture

300 kW Charging

300 kW is faster than the Tesla Model S (250 kW), BMW i7 (195 kW), and Mercedes EQS (200 kW). At a compatible 350-kW DC charger, the Air adds ~300 miles in under 30 minutes.

The Lucid Air uses an 800V electrical architecture (Lucid calls it 900V) — enabling 300-kW DC fast charging. At 300 kW, the Air adds approximately 200 miles of range in 22 minutes — faster than any other luxury EV sedan.

06

Sapphire Performance

1.89 Seconds

0–60 in 1.89 seconds. This is faster than a Formula 1 car from a standing start at low speed. The Sapphire has run the quarter-mile in 8.95 seconds — the fastest documented time for any production vehicle.

The Air Sapphire delivers 1,234 hp via three motors — a dedicated motor per rear wheel plus one front motor. 0–60 in 1.89 seconds and quarter-mile in 8.95 seconds are verified production car records. The Sapphire's torque-vectoring via individual rear motors creates handling that matches purpose-built track cars.

FAQ

What is the 2026 Lucid Air starting price?
Air Pure starts at $69,900. Touring ~$89,900. Grand Touring ~$138,000. Air Sapphire ~$249,000. Prices exclude destination.
What is the Lucid Air range?
Grand Touring: 516 miles EPA — the longest EPA-certified range of any production EV ever made. Pure: 410 miles. Touring: 425 miles. Sapphire: 427 miles.
How fast does the Air charge?
300 kW DC fast charging on all Air models. At a compatible 350-kW station, the Air adds approximately 200 miles in 22 minutes — the fastest charging of any luxury EV sedan.
What is the Air Sapphire?
The tri-motor performance flagship — 1,234 hp, 0–60 in 1.89 seconds, 8.95-second quarter mile. Individual rear motors enable torque vectoring. Starts at $249,000.
Is Lucid a reliable car brand?
Better than the early launch suggests — 2024+ production has significantly improved build quality and software maturity. Still below established luxury brands in terms of reliability data depth. Extended warranty recommended.
Is the a good value in 2026?
The delivers competitive value in its segment. We recommend cross-shopping alternatives and checking current incentive programs before purchasing. Verify all current pricing and incentives with your local Lucid dealer.
