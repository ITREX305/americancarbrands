Brand Overview

John Hennessey founded Hennessey Performance Engineering in 1991 with a focus on high-performance engine modifications. Over three decades, Hennessey has grown from a Supra tuner to America's premier hypercar builder. The company modifies vehicles across brands — Ford, Dodge, Chevrolet, GMC, Jeep, RAM — while also manufacturing the ground-up Venom F5 hypercar in limited production.

The Venom F5: Named for the most destructive tornado category, the Venom F5 is powered by Hennessey's own 6.6L twin-turbo V8 ('Fury') producing 1,817 hp. Production is limited to 24 coupes and 12 roadsters — all customer-allocated. Controversy: Hennessey has a documented history of customer disputes over build delays and upgrade claims — buyers are advised to research thoroughly and work through established channels. Warranty: Hennessey offers its own warranty on modifications; base vehicle warranties may be affected. Verify specifics before contracting.

Buying Tips 2026

Hennessey Performance has a polarizing reputation — world-class engineering capability combined with documented customer service controversies. For Venom F5 purchases, engage directly with the factory and request current build status documentation. For HPE upgrade packages on donor vehicles, request written specifications, delivery timelines, and warranty terms in writing. Reference community forums (Hennessey Owners Group, relevant enthusiast boards) for recent customer experiences before committing.

Before You Buy — Checklist

1. For Venom F5: request current production position and delivery timeline in writing. 2. For HPE packages: get full written specs, timeline, and warranty terms before any deposit. 3. FAQ

Is the Hennessey Venom F5 the fastest car in the world?
As of 2026, the Bugatti Chiron Super Sport 300+ holds the production car speed record at 304.773 mph (officially). The Venom F5 has recorded runs exceeding 270 mph and continues high-speed testing targeting 300+ mph. Whether it has officially broken the record — verify with current automotive press.
How many Venom F5s are being built?
Hennessey plans to produce 24 Venom F5 coupes and 12 open-top Roadsters — 36 total. All units are customer-allocated. The coupe starts at approximately $2.1M, the Roadster at approximately $2.7M.
Is Hennessey Performance reputable?
Hennessey is technically capable — the Venom F5 is a legitimate engineering achievement. However, Hennessey has a documented history of customer complaints over delays, unfulfilled upgrades, and communication issues. Research recent owner experiences thoroughly before any financial commitment.
Do Hennessey upgrades void the factory warranty?
In most cases, yes — high-performance engine modifications like HPE packages typically void the base manufacturer's powertrain warranty on affected components. Hennessey offers its own warranty on modifications. Verify specifics in writing before purchasing any upgrade package.
Where is Hennessey Performance located?
Hennessey Performance Engineering is headquartered and operates from Sealy, Texas, approximately 45 miles west of Houston. The facility includes an engineering center, build shop, and private test track.
